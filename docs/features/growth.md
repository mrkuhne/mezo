# Growth — Daily Quests, Activity Log & Gamified Progression

> Surfaces as the **"Napi küldetések"** + **"Tevékenységnapló"** cards on the **`/me/growth` page's `Skillek` tab** (Growth is a full-page sibling of the **Én** tab since Design 2.0), and on the **Nap** tab as a `Küldetések` mosaic tile → the dedicated **`/nap/kuldetesek`** page. Both quest surfaces read the same query; the activity log is additionally reachable from the app-wide quick-log FAB (`Napló → Aktivitás`). The wider economy surfaces through the **Én hub's identity hero** (level ring, `Lv`/`XP`/🔥 streak/🪙 coins) and the Growth page itself. **The (E3) "Growth — heti" card on Insights' Weekly tab is RETIRED (`mezo-p2tr`)** — `GrowthWeekCard.tsx`/`growthWeekApi.ts` are deleted along with the Weekly tab; the Progression `GET /api/progression/growth-week/{date}` endpoint survives with no FE consumer. **Status: ✅ E1 + E2 + E3 + the Growth page done** — daily-quest core shipped 2026-07-11 (`mezo-df7q`, E1); the growth layer (full 8-skill LIFE band + free-text activity log with AI categorization + the GROWTH quest slot + computed discipline/consistency traits) shipped 2026-07-11 (`mezo-jzca`, E2); the **life-integrations polish** (savings aggregate + weekly growth-week aggregate/endpoint + proactive digests + adaptive quest difficulty + companion flavor copy) shipped 2026-07-11 (`mezo-6ng8`, E3); the **Me Growth page** (dedicated `/me/growth` route + quest/activity history reads + a derived-on-read achievements economy; the three former Profil progression cards — athletic radar/muscle levels/LIFE — consolidated into one summary card and **radars retired app-wide**) shipped 2026-07-12 (`mezo-rmhr`). **E4** (shop/coins) remains, under umbrella epic `mezo-52vz`.

## 1. Summary

**Growth** is the gamified personal-growth layer. Two loops feed one XP economy:

1. **Daily side quests** — **3 slots** (BODY + FUELBIO + GROWTH), selected deterministically from a static catalog, completed **by derived evaluation over already-logged data** (never self-claimed), granting XP through the existing progression economy.
2. **Activity log (E2)** — a **free-text mini-journal**: the user types what they did ("olvastam 30 percet", "átraktam 50 ezret megtakarításba"), the **companion cheap-tier LLM proposes** a LIFE skill + XP, and the **server disposes** — clamping/capping the amount deterministically before it lands on one of the **8 LIFE skills**. A confident entry also completes the day's matching open **activity-mode** GROWTH quest.

The psychological contract is [ADR 0010](../decisions/0010-gamified-growth-xp-feedback-not-payment.md): *XP is feedback, not payment* — quests are offers, uncompleted quests expire quietly (no failure state), one reroll/day preserves autonomy, quest XP stays a garnish (15–40/quest), and the **behavior traits are computed/mirrored back, never self-claimed**. The LLM only ever *proposes*; every XP amount that actually lands is deterministic (catalog value for quests, `[xpMin,xpMax]`-clamped + per-skill/per-day-capped for activities).

Status per layer: **backend** ✅ (`feature/quest` + `feature/activity` + progression `QUEST`/`ACTIVITY` sources + full `LIFE` band + `TraitCalculator` + E3's `GrowthWeekService` aggregate, `ActivityLedgerSource` savings port, `QuestSelector` adaptive banding, `QuestFlavor` companion copy, `GrowthDigestBlock` proactive digests), **FE real** ✅ (quest day + reroll, activity log/categorize, the `/me/growth` page — all-band skills, 30-day journal, badges/perks — incl. the savings stat, reward-toast level-ups since `mezo-k5sa`; **the Insights Weekly `GrowthWeekCard` is RETIRED**, `mezo-p2tr`), **FE mock** ✅ (static seed day, deterministic mock classifier, seed history + 4/9 badge state).

**Design 2.0 changed the render layer, not the economy.** The `mezo-d20` redesign re-faced Growth onto the Mozaik tile language (`mezo-d20.6.5`) and moved the quest surface from a Today chip+sheet to a Nap tile + its own page (`mezo-d20.2.4`), then deleted the dissolved app shell in the F8 cleanup (`mezo-d20.9.1`). **The XP model itself is untouched**: the 3-slot catalog, derived evaluation, the clamp/cap arithmetic, the LIFE taxonomy, the traits, the achievement derivation, every endpoint and every table are exactly what they were. [ADR 0010](../decisions/0010-gamified-growth-xp-feedback-not-payment.md) — *XP is feedback, not payment* — still governs every one of them, and the new faces were built to it: quests are still offers with no failure state, misses are never red, nothing self-completes from the UI. The two Design 2.0 ADRs that describe the new surface are [0032](../decisions/0032-five-tab-ia-dissolved-section-shells.md) (five tabs, dissolved shells, full-page siblings, the quick-log FAB) and [0033](../decisions/0033-mozaik-2-tile-language.md) (the tile mosaic, clay icons, the `--mz-*` tokens, entrance choreography). Driving spec: [`2026-07-11-gamified-growth-quests-design.md`](../superpowers/specs/2026-07-11-gamified-growth-quests-design.md) (E3 = §10); plans: [E1 quest-core](../superpowers/plans/2026-07-11-gamified-growth-e1-quest-core.md) · [E2 growth-layer](../superpowers/plans/2026-07-11-gamified-growth-e2-growth-layer.md) · [E3 life-polish](../superpowers/plans/2026-07-11-gamified-growth-e3-life-polish.md).

## 2. User-facing behavior

- **"Napi küldetések" on Growth and Nap:** the Growth **hub** (`/me/growth`, `GrowthHubPage`, `mezo-rmi0.1`) surfaces today's quests + activities as the **Ma-csík** (`features/me/components/MaStrip.tsx`) — a header button (`Ma · {done}/{n} küldetés` + a sage `+{xp} XP` chip + `›`) that navigates to `/nap/kuldetesek`, over a wrapping chip row (no horizontal scroll): one chip per today's quest — `completed` = sage wash + filled ✓, `open` = a neutral outline + empty circle, `expired`/`rerolled` = a dashed outline at 50% opacity reading `{cím} · csendben lejárt` (`aria-disabled`) — **never terracotta, never a countdown**. **There is no explicit "done" mutation anywhere in the domain** (a DERIVED quest closes itself off the logs), so tapping an *open* chip branches on `completionMode`: **`ACTIVITY`** opens the existing `ActivityLogSheet` with the quest attached (the same `Naplózz` flow, quest banner included); **`DERIVED`** navigates to `/nap/kuldetesek`, where the quest's own honest state line (`folyamatban · a logjaidból záródik magától`) and the `Csere` reroll live — the strip itself never claims a derived quest is "in progress" beyond the chip's neutral outline. A `＋ Tevékenység` chip closes the row and opens the same `ActivityLogSheet` with no quest attached; a saved, categorized activity renders back as a levendula `✎ {name} · +{xp}` chip. An empty day renders one honest line ("Ma még nincs küldetés — a reggeli briefinggel jön. Tevékenységet közben is logolhatsz.") plus the `＋ Tevékenység` chip, never `0/0`. The completed-quest level-up toast (`buildQuestRewardToast`, consume-once) moved verbatim from `DailyQuestsCard`'s effect into `MaStrip`. **`DailyQuestsCard`/`ActivityLogCard` no longer mount on Growth** — see §9. **On Nap the surface is unchanged: a `Küldetések` mosaic tile → its own page** (`/nap/kuldetesek`, `NapKuldetesekPage`, `mezo-d20.2.4`): the tile's line is `{done}/{total}` plus the day's remaining `+N XP` (or `· kész ✓`), and the page renders the same `useDailyQuests`/`useQuestActions` read as quest cards with an XP pill, the reroll affordance and the same metric-specific smart actions (`questAction`) the old sheet had — plus an honest per-quest state line (`folyamatban · a logjaidból záródik magától`, or `· az edzésből` for the gym metric) that says out loud how a quest closes. `DailyQuestsSheet` survives as the **Nap hub's** in-place quest sheet; nothing was dropped, only re-homed.
- **Reroll ("Csere"):** offered quests show a `Csere` button on both surfaces while the daily reroll budget (1) lasts; it swaps the quest for the next eligible catalog candidate in the same slot. On Nap the surface refreshes the affected row plus its remaining-budget summary in place, without unwinding to the hub.
- **Derived completion is passive:** the user never taps "done" — logging the underlying thing (weight, water, check-ins, sleep, the planned workout, protein, **a meal cooked from their own recipe**) completes the DERIVED quest on the next day read (or the nightly cron). A completion detected by the current read builds and emits a DS reward toast exactly once (`buildQuestRewardToast`, eyebrow „Küldetés" — `mezo-k5sa`, see [habit.md §2](habit.md)/[today.md §2](today.md); no longer the full-screen overlay).
- **Activity logging on Growth (E2)** — the Ma-csík's `✎` chips + its trailing `＋ Tevékenység` chip (above) are Growth's activity surface now; the standalone `ActivityLogCard.tsx` no longer mounts there. Since the quick-log FAB shipped (`mezo-d20.1.6`) the same `ActivityLogSheet` is also one tap from **any** screen: FAB → `Napló` → `Aktivitás`. Same sheet, same write path, same caps — a second door, not a second flow. Opened from a quest chip it shows the quest banner; opened from `＋ Tevékenység` or the FAB it starts blank. ### Account progression (`mezo-k7rn` FE-first 2026-07-18 → `mezo-huzd` real backend 2026-07-25; the `AppHero` header that carried it is deleted, `mezo-d20.9.1`)

A distinct-but-adjacent layer from the daily-quest/activity-log economy above: an **account-wide** XP/level/coin/streak/title system. It used to be surfaced by the shared **`AppHero`** header on all 5 section shells (Today/Train/Fuel/Me/Insights — the last joined via the compact-header redesign, `mezo-ugqb`, 2026-07-18). **Those shells and `AppHero` with them are gone** ([ADR 0032](../decisions/0032-five-tab-ia-dissolved-section-shells.md)): the account profile now lives in exactly one place, the **Én hub's identity hero**, and the ⚡ quest counter it carried is the Nap hub's `Küldetések` tile. The ledger below is entirely unchanged by that — only its display moved. **Not the same thing as the §1 "E4 (shop/coins)" umbrella** (`mezo-52vz`, still deferred) — that epic was about a shop/coin economy layered onto THIS doc's quest/activity XP; the account-wide coin/title system is a separate ledger under its own driving issues (`mezo-k7rn` FE, `mezo-huzd` backend). Driving spec: [`2026-07-18-gamified-header-design.md`](../superpowers/specs/2026-07-18-gamified-header-design.md).

- **Backend (`feature/gamification`, `mezo-huzd`) — real, switch-gated on `mezo.feature.gamification.enabled`.** Three owned tables: `gamification_profile` (one row/owner — `coins`, `streakDays`, `streakSavers`, `lastStreakDate`, `equippedTitleKey`, `accountLevel`), `coin_event` (append-only ledger — `reason`, `amount`, `occurredOn`, idempotency key on `(created_by, reason, source_ref_id)`), `owned_title` (shop purchases only; ladder titles are level-derived, never rows). `GamificationService`/`GamificationController` serve `GET /api/gamification/profile` (→ `totalXp` = Σ every skill's `cumulativeXp`, `SkillProgressRepository.sumCumulativeXp` — the SAME server-side total this doc's Skillek tab FE-sums — walked through the **account level curve**, `AccountLevelCurve.levelFor`, `xpToNext(n) = 80 + 40·(n−1)`, ported 1:1 from the FE mock; plus `coins`/`streakDays`/`streakSavers`/`equippedTitleKey`/`ownedTitleKeys` straight off the profile row, a missing row → all-zero/default-title, never a 404), **`GET /api/gamification/day/{date}`** (the **ritual Harvest read**: `xpBySource` groups the day's `level_up_event` rows by `sourceType` in a fixed `GYM/RUN/SPORT/QUEST/ACTIVITY/HABIT` order keyed by the new `occurred_on` business date below, plus the day's `coin_event` rows/total and the live streak — honest zeros, never a 404), and the three mutations `POST /api/gamification/title/{key}/buy|equip` + `POST /api/gamification/saver/buy` (409 `GAMIFICATION_TITLE_OWNED`/`_NOT_SHOP`/`_LOCKED`/`COINS_INSUFFICIENT`/`SAVER_LIMIT`, 404 `GAMIFICATION_TITLE_UNKNOWN`). `buyTitle` auto-equips, mirroring the FE mock's prior behavior.
- **Server-side coin awards ride the SAME idempotent tail every XP source already uses.** `AccountProgressPort` (`feature/progression/AccountProgressPort.java`) is a one-directional hook (progression calls out, gamification implements — an `ObjectProvider`, absent no-op when the switch is off) that `ProgressionService.award(...)`'s shared tail (§3) fires **once per NEWLY-created `level_up_event`** — never on the idempotent replay path, so re-processing a GYM/RUN/SPORT/QUEST/ACTIVITY/HABIT award is a safe no-op end to end. `GamificationAccountAdapter` implements the port (runs INSIDE the award's own transaction, `Propagation.MANDATORY`, so a rollback of the XP grant rolls the coin/streak side back too) and fans the event into streak rollover (below) + `+10` coins per completed QUEST (`+20` bonus on the day's 3rd quest) + `+50` coins per account level crossed (walked from the profile's stored `accountLevel` to the freshly-recomputed one, so a multi-level jump pays out per level, not once). **This is genuinely new reach for QUEST/ACTIVITY/HABIT**: those sources already moved `totalXp` (they add `cumulativeXp`, so the real-mode account level was never ghost), but before this slice real-mode coins/streak/titles only ever moved off GYM/RUN/SPORT — completing a quest or logging an activity/habit now visibly pays coins and keeps the streak alive too.
- **`level_up_event` gained a business-date column** (`occurred_on`, migration `202607241130_mezo-huzd_level_up_event_occurred_on.sql`), set explicitly by every `award(...)` call — never defaulted. GYM/RUN/SPORT still stamp `LocalDate.now()` (grant date = event date); QUEST/ACTIVITY/HABIT now carry their OWN row-borne date (`QuestSignal.occurredOn`/`ActivitySignal.occurredOn`/`HabitSignal.occurredOn` — the quest's `questDate`, the activity's `occurredOn`, the habit's `habitDate`) instead of implicitly "now", which is what lets the Harvest day-read and the streak rollover key off the ledger's own business date rather than the insert instant (`occurred_at`, unchanged — still the grant timestamp). Habits can only be checked for *today* in v1 (`requireManualToday`), so `occurred_on`/`occurred_at` always coincide for HABIT today — the plumbing is future-proofing for whenever a source gains backdating, not an observable behavior change yet.
- **Read-time honest streak** (`GamificationService.streakAlive`): `lastStreakDate ∈ {today, yesterday}` → alive, so a fresh day still shows yesterday's living streak with no cron. **Rollover** (`GamificationAccountAdapter.applyStreakRollover`, idempotent per `occurredOn` — a second same-day award no-ops since `lastStreakDate` already equals it): gap of 1 day → `streakDays+1`; gap of 2 with a saver banked → the saver auto-consumes (a `saver_used` `+0` coin_event) and the streak survives; anything else → reset to 1. Crossing a configured streak-day milestone in the rollover pays its coin bonus.
- **`GamificationProperties`** (`mezo.gamification`, `@ConfigurationProperties`) — the only place these numbers live: `saverPrice` 200, `maxSavers` 2, `levelUpCoins` 50, `questCoins` 10, `all3Coins` 20, `milestoneCoins` `{7:50, 30:150, 100:500}`. Sinks: shop titles (100–600 🪙) + the streak saver.
- **GYM's `max_strength` PR bonus now actually fires (medal collection, `mezo-wp6n`).** The GYM-path XP engine's `prBonusXp` component (`ProgressionService.applyGym`, `_platform-api-backend.md` §4e) previously paid only once in an account's lifetime — the first-ever weighted session — despite `ProgressionProperties.Gym.prBonusXp` being documented as "bonus when e1RM beats prior best." It now pays `prBonusXp × recordMedalCount` into `max_strength`, where `recordMedalCount` is the just-finished session's count of `RECORD`-tier medals (`WEIGHT`/`REPS_AT_WEIGHT`/`E1RM`/`SESSION_VOLUME`), computed live off `MedalService.forSession` by `GymSignalCalculator` — see [train.md](train.md) §2 `Medálok` / §4 and [ADR 0016](../decisions/0016-medals-derived-not-materialized.md) for the medal engine itself. A second, new `GymSignal` component, `targetMedalCount` (per-set prescription hits, `TARGET_HIT` tier), pays `targetMedalXp × min(targetMedalCount, targetMedalCap)` into `strength_endurance` (new `mezo.progression.gym.target-medal-xp` = 5 / `target-medal-cap` = 12 properties). ## 3. Architecture & data flow

**Quests:** `DailyQuestsCard` → `useDailyQuests(date)` / `useQuestActions(date)` (from `@/data/hooks`) → mock: `questMock.ts` seed · real: `questApi.ts` → `GET /api/quest/day/{date}` → `QuestService.getDay` → lazy `QuestSelector.generate` (today only, guarded against the morning-cron race) + `QuestService.evaluateAndFinalize` (complete/expire + `ProgressionService.applyQuest`) → `daily_quest` table.

- **Generation is deterministic over 3 slots** (`QuestSelector.SLOTS` = BODY, FUELBIO, GROWTH): seeded by `(userId, date, slot)` hash over the eligible catalog pool — filters: slot, day type (planned gym template via `WorkoutService.findPlannedTemplateForDate` → GYM, else REST), goal-prescription requirement, per-key cooldown (yields to availability), distinct metrics per day. No LLM anywhere in the economy (ADR 0010).
- **Evaluation is DERIVED-guarded** (`QuestService.evaluateAndFinalize`): only `MODE_DERIVED` quests auto-complete via `QuestEvaluator.satisfied` (pure reads — check-in count, weight-log presence, water sum, sleep duration, fuel-day protein, gym done-dates, **own-recipe meal**, **intention focus set** — the `growth_intention` quest, `mezo-a686`). **`MODE_ACTIVITY` quests never auto-complete** — they complete only through the activity-write hook, and otherwise **expire quietly** like any offered quest once the day passes.
- **XP path (quests):** completion → `ProgressionService.applyQuest(QuestSignal)` → the shared idempotent `award(...)` tail (`source_type=QUEST`, `source_ref_id=questId`) → `skill_progress`/`level_up_event`; gated by `ObjectProvider<ProgressionGate>`.

**Activity log (E2):** `ActivityLogCard`/`ActivityLogSheet` → `useActivities(date)` / `useActivityActions(date)` (from `@/data/hooks`, dual-mode via `useDualQuery`) → `activityApi.ts` → `POST /api/activity` (+ `GET /api/activity/day/{date}`, `POST /api/activity/{id}/category`) → `ActivityService`.

- **Write flow** (`ActivityService.create`): persist the entry → `ActivityClassifier.classify` (companion cheap tier) proposes `{skillKey, confidence, xpSuggestion(5..25), durationMin?, amountHuf?}` → the suggestion is **clamped** into `[xpMin,xpMax]` and stored in `xp_suggested`; **confident** (`skillKey` set, a LIFE key, `confidence ≥ confidenceThreshold` 0.6) → set `skill_key` + `categorized_by = AI` → **`grantXp`** awards `min(xpSuggested, perSkillDailyCap − skillUsedToday, dailyCap − dayUsedToday)` and, when > 0, calls `ProgressionService.applyActivity` (source `ACTIVITY`, idempotent per entry id) → then **`completeMatchingActivityQuest`** completes the day's first open activity-mode quest whose LIFE skill matches, stamping `source_activity_id` (quest XP rides on top). **Low confidence / classifier off** → the entry lands **uncategorized** (`skill_key` null), **no XP yet**.
- **Categorize / override** (`ActivityService.categorize`, `POST /api/activity/{id}/category`): validates the key against `ProgressionTaxonomy.LIFE`. First categorization of an uncategorized entry (`xp_awarded == 0`) → `grantXp` within the day's remaining caps. **Override** of an already-awarded entry → `ProgressionService.moveActivityXp(from, to, xp)` — a **direct skill-row adjustment** (decrement the old LIFE row, increment/create the new one, recompute levels), **no new `level_up_event`**. Either path also runs `completeMatchingActivityQuest`. `categorized_by` becomes `USER`.
- **Response shape** (`ActivityWriteResponse`): `{ entry, completedQuest?, levelUps[] }` — 0–2 level-up payloads (the activity award and/or the completed quest's award). The FE surfaces the first payload with a level-up through the global overlay.

**Traits (E2, read-time):** `ProgressionService.getProfile` calls `TraitCalculator.traits(createdBy, today)` (`TraitCalculator.java`):
- **Discipline** (`disciplinePct`, nullable): a 28-day (`DISCIPLINE_WINDOW_DAYS`) completion ratio, the **average of the available components** — planned-vs-done training (`TrainingCommitmentSource.commitmentStats`, implemented in train by `TrainingCommitmentCalculator`) blended with closed-quest `completed / (completed + expired)` (`QuestLedgerSource.closedQuestStats`, implemented in quest by `QuestLedgerAdapter`, consumed via `ObjectProvider` since the quest switch may be off). **Null** (not 0) when there are no commitments in the window.
- **Consistency** (`consistencyWeeks`): the current streak of consecutive ISO weeks with **≥ 4 active days** (`ACTIVE_DAYS_PER_WEEK`), an active day = any XP-earning row in `level_up_event.occurred_at` (`findOccurredAtSince`, 400-day horizon). The **running week can't break the streak** — it counts only once it already meets the bar (grace, mirrors the robustness streak's tone).

Both ports (`TrainingCommitmentSource`, `QuestLedgerSource`) keep the dependency **one-directional** (train/quest → progression, never back — `feature_slices_are_cycle_free`), the same pattern as `RobustnessSource`.

**E3 — life integrations (`mezo-6ng8`):** three read-time aggregates + two generation-time behaviors, all rule-based (no LLM in the economy — ADR 0010; the LLM only ever rewrites flavor copy):

- **Savings port + 30-day profile stat:** a new `ActivityLedgerSource` port (`feature/progression/ActivityLedgerSource.java`, `Stats(entries, savingsHuf)`) is implemented in `feature/activity` by `ActivityLedgerAdapter` (`ACTIVITY_SWITCH`-gated) — it sums `extracted.amountHuf` over the window's `skill_key='financial'` entries. `ProgressionService.getProfile` consumes it via `ObjectProvider` and adds **`savingsHuf30d`** (trailing 30 days, `now-29..now`; **null** when the activity feature is off) to `ProgressionProfileResponse` — the Me `GrowthSummaryCard` + Growth-page LIFE-band "Megtakarítás (30 nap)" row.
- **Growth-week aggregate + endpoint:** `GrowthWeekService.growthWeek(userId, anyDayOfWeek)` keys the **ISO week** (`anyDayOfWeek.with(MONDAY)`, system zone — the `TraitCalculator` precedent) and composes: quest `completed`/`closed` (= completed+expired) from `QuestLedgerSource.closedQuestStats`, **LIFE XP** = the sum of `level_up_event` payload gains with `kind='LIFE'` occurring in `[weekStart 00:00, weekEnd+1 00:00)`, and activity count + savings from `ActivityLedgerSource` — both ledger ports via `ObjectProvider`, so an off switch yields **honest zeros, never an error**. Served by `GET /api/progression/growth-week/{date}` (§4); also consumed by the proactive `GrowthDigestBlock` (§5).
- **Adaptive difficulty banding** (`QuestSelector.allowedDifficulties`): per slot, count the trailing-`window-days` (28) **closed** rows for that slot — `completed` vs `expired`. `< min-sample` (5) closed → all tiers `{1,2,3}` (v1 behavior, insufficient signal); ratio `completed/closed ≥ high-ratio` (0.85) → `{1,2,3}`; `≤ low-ratio` (0.50) → `{1}`; else `{1,2}`. In `pick`/`replacement` the banded pool is filtered from the eligible pool, but **difficulty yields to availability** (empty banded pool → fall back to the full eligible pool), and only **then** does the existing **cooldown yield to banded**. Reads only terminal statuses — offered/rerolled rows never count.
- **Flavor copy flow** (`QuestFlavor.rewrite`, gated `QUEST` + `COMPANION` + `mezo.quest.flavor.enabled`): called by `QuestJob.runGenerate` via `ObjectProvider` right after `QuestSelector.generate` (the **morning cron path only**). It builds one prompt (marker `KULDETES-IZESITES-FELADAT`) listing the fresh quests, calls `CompanionLlm.complete` (cheap tier), and expects a strict-JSON array of `{title, why}` with **exactly** the same count/order. Any failure — LLM error, unparseable, count mismatch, a blank/over-length entry (`title ≤ 160`, `why ≤ 600`) — **quietly keeps the catalog copy** (the offer is already correct); valid entries are persisted per-quest. `FakeCompanionLlm` mirrors the marker and reads a `[fake-quest-flavor:[…]]` sentinel (default `[]` = no rewrite), so ITs are provider-free.

- **Dual-mode seams:** quests use the `useChallenges` manual shape (`levelUps`/`rerollsLeft` ride beside the list); activities use `useDualQuery` (real → `[]` while unresolved, never the mock seed). `useActivityActions` invalidates `['activities', date]` + `['dailyQuests', date]` + `['progressionProfile']` on a real write so the activity card, the quest card, and the Profil `GrowthSummaryCard` all refresh. Mock mode has a deterministic classifier (`activityHooks.ts:mockWrite` → always `learning`, 15 XP) so the whole flow is demoable offline. **E3's growth-week FE read is RETIRED (`mezo-p2tr`)** — it used to ride `useWeekly()` in the Insights domain (`weeklyHooks.ts`, real-only `['insightsWeekly','growth', start]` query → `growthWeekApi.get`); the backend `GrowthWeekService`/endpoint is untouched, just unread from the FE.
- **Growth page reads (`mezo-rmhr`):** the `/me/growth` Napló + Kitüntetések tabs add three `useDualQuery` reads — **`useQuestHistory(from,to)`** (`data/quest/questHooks.ts`, `['questHistory', from, to]` → `GET /api/quest/history` → `QuestService.history`, rerolled rows filtered server-side) + **`useActivityHistory(from,to)`** (`data/activity/activityHooks.ts`, `['activityHistory', from, to]` → `GET /api/activity/history`), merged by the pure **`buildGrowthJournal`** (`features/me/logic/growthJournal.ts`); and **`useAchievements()`** (`data/progression/progressionHooks.ts`, `['achievements']` → `GET /api/progression/achievements` → `AchievementService`). All barrel-exported, honest-empty in real mode, with deterministic mock seeds (`questMock.mockQuestHistory` / `activityMock.mockActivityHistory` / `achievementsMock` mirroring the mockup's 4/9 badge state) + MSW defaults. The Skillek tab + hero read the existing `useProgressionProfile()` (no new profile field — Össz XP is FE-summed).

**Achievements (`mezo-rmhr`, read-time):** `AchievementService.achievements(createdBy)` (`feature/progression/service/AchievementService.java`) derives **9 badges on read** from the EXISTING seams — no new tables, deterministic, retroactive. Inputs: `QuestLedgerSource.closedQuestStats` (all-time `completed` → first_quest/quests_10/quests_50), `ActivityLedgerSource.stats` (all-time `entries` → first_activity + `savingsHuf` → savings_100k) — both via `ObjectProvider` so a switch-off port reports `current=0` honestly —, `TraitCalculator.traits(...).consistencyWeeks` (rhythm_4w), and `SkillProgressRepository` LIFE rows (all_life_active / life_lv5 / life_xp_10k). Each badge is `{key, icon, name, achieved = current >= target, current, target}`. **Perks** come straight from `PerkUnlockRepository.findByCreatedByOrderByUnlockedAtDesc` joined with `PerkCatalog` copy. **No unlock dates on the badges** (there is no unlock ledger — §9).

## 6. How to use it (consume)

```ts
import { useDailyQuests, useQuestActions, useActivities, useActivityActions } from '@/data/hooks'
const { quests, levelUps, rerollsLeft } = useDailyQuests(date)         // date = 'YYYY-MM-DD'
const { reroll } = useQuestActions(date)
const { data: entries } = useActivities(date)                          // ActivityEntry[]
const { logActivity, categorize, pending } = useActivityActions(date)
await logActivity('Olvastam 30 percet')                                // → ActivityWriteResult (entry + completedQuest? + levelUps)
await categorize(entryId, 'learning')                                  // pick/override a LIFE skill
```
`quests` excludes rerolled rows; `quests[i].completionMode === 'ACTIVITY'` marks the `Naplózz`-affordance quests. `levelUps` on the quest read is non-empty only on the read that performed the completion. `logActivity`/`categorize` return the write result — feed `result.levelUps` into a reward toast (`buildQuestRewardToast`, `Naplózva` eyebrow — the sheet does this) and read `result.completedQuest` for the "quest completed" banner. Real mode returns the empty day while loading (no seed fallback). Query keys: `['dailyQuests', date]`, `['activities', date]`.

## 7. How to extend it

- **New LIFE skill:** add the token to `ProgressionTaxonomy.LIFE` (backend, taxonomy order) **and** the matching `{key, name, icon, clayIcon}` to `LIFE_SKILLS` in `frontend/src/features/progression/logic/levelUpMeta.ts` — the two lists must agree in order and membership. **`clayIcon` (a `ClayIconName`, e.g. `i-life-tanulas`) is a required field on every `LIFE_SKILLS` entry since F7.4** — it is what `GrowthSkillsPage` renders per LIFE row (`ClayIcon name={m.clayIcon}`), and if the key ever fails the `lifeMeta` lookup the row falls back to the same **two-letter-initials** treatment the athletic/muscle rows use, never the emoji (a test bans the 8 LIFE emojis from the rendered output — `icon` is data only, never UI, on any Growth surface). The classifier prompt (`ActivityClassifier.CLASSIFY_PROMPT`) enumerates the keys, so extend it too, and add the new key to the `LifeSkillKey` FE type. The Growth pages' LIFE `SkillBandCard` + journal render whatever `LIFE_SKILLS` carries, so no card change is needed (the `all_life_active` badge target `8` in `AchievementService` would need bumping if the LIFE band grows).
- **New GROWTH quest:** add a catalog entry with `slot: "GROWTH"` + a `mode`. An **ACTIVITY** quest (`mode: "ACTIVITY"`, `metric: "activity_match"`) completes via a matching activity-log entry — no evaluator work needed. A **DERIVED** quest needs a new `metric` case in `QuestEvaluator.satisfied` + a label in `QuestDisplay.targetLabel`. Quest XP must stay in the 15–40 band (loader fails fast otherwise).
- **New derived metric:** add the `case` to `QuestEvaluator.satisfied` reading the owning feature's repository/service (pure reads only — honest completion). `own_recipe_meal` is the template for a Fuel-sourced metric.
- **New achievement badge (`mezo-rmhr`):** append a `badge(key, icon, name, current, target)` row to the ordered list in `AchievementService.achievements` — source `current` from an EXISTING seam (`QuestLedgerSource`/`ActivityLedgerSource`/`TraitCalculator`/`SkillProgressRepository`), NO new port/table. The generic `BadgesCard` renders it with zero FE change; update `AchievementsApiIT` + the mock seed `data/progression/achievementsMock.ts` + the MSW default. Unlock **dates** are deliberately out of scope in v1 (badges are derived on read — §9).
- **Tune the activity economy:** all guardrails are `ActivityProperties` (`mezo.activity.*`) — never hardcode XP/caps/thresholds (`configuration_conventions.md`).
- **Tune adaptive difficulty (E3):** `mezo.quest.adaptive.*` (`window-days`/`min-sample`/`high-ratio`/`low-ratio`, bound + validated by `QuestProperties.Adaptive`). To widen/narrow a band edit the ratios; to make difficulty static again set `min-sample` above any realistic closed-count (all slots then fall to `{1,2,3}`). A new difficulty tier needs a wider catalog `difficulty` range (the loader currently pins 1..3) **and** a band that admits it.
- **Disable/adjust flavor copy (E3):** flip `mezo.quest.flavor.enabled` off to keep catalog copy everywhere; the rewrite is otherwise silent-fail-safe. It runs **only** on `QuestJob.runGenerate` (via `ObjectProvider<QuestFlavor>`) — do NOT wire it into the lazy read or reroll paths (that would rewrite copy the user has already seen). Extend the prompt/limits in `QuestFlavor` (`TITLE_MAX`/`WHY_MAX`), never let it touch targets/XP/skill (ADR 0010).
- House standards: contract-first (`api_contract_conventions.md`), backend per `docs/references/*.md`, dual-mode hook recipe in [`_platform-data-layer.md`](_platform-data-layer.md).

- **Mock fidelity note (mezo-rmhr fix wave):** `progressionProfileMock.athletic` carries the full 12-entry band (incl. `robustness`) to mirror the real profile response, so all-band row counts and FE-summed XP literals match between modes.

Mock mode does not: the account total is a separate seeded counter (`gamificationProfileMock.totalXp`) advanced only by the ten flat-XP mock mutations listed in §2's "Account progression" subsection (incl. the habit manual check) — quest/activity completions still do not feed it in mock mode. This is a new orphan, not the old resolved one above — the two files, `DailyQuestList` (still consumed by `DailyQuestsSheet`) and `ActivityLogSheet` (still the live write surface everywhere, incl. the FAB and `MaStrip` itself) are unaffected.
- **Implementation flag — `PageHero` deliberately leaves `sub` unused on the Growth sub-pages (`mezo-rmi0.1`).** `PageHero` DOES accept a `sub?: string` prop (`.mz-hero-sb`, used by ~14 other sub-pages for a short one-line caption) — this is a design-recipe choice, not an API gap. The shared subpage-hero recipe (design-2.0 implementation spec §3 / handoff §10) is "title, then icon + big number in one row, no subtitle theater": a long multi-clause line like the prototype's `sb` (e.g. `három sáv · 8 LIFE · 12 atlétikus · 13 izom`) doesn't fit that recipe's one-line-caption intent, so Skillek/Napló/Kitüntetések route that content into a `StatStrip` or the `PageBody principle` line instead. - **Gotcha — Rutin's counter-cells gotcha moved with the page (`mezo-rmi0.1`, follow-up `mezo-11nm`, `mezo-3zue.3`).** The 30-cell counter-vs-calendar caveat this bullet used to carry now lives on [habit.md](habit.md), alongside `RutinHubPage` (`/me/rutin`), which absorbed the former `GrowthRutinPage`'s `.gr-covtile` cell-strips and the routine editor both.
- **[ADR 0010](../decisions/0010-gamified-growth-xp-feedback-not-payment.md)** is the contract every extension must respect (no failure states, no XP-gated content, no gambling mechanics, coins only with a shop, **the LLM proposes but the server disposes**, traits computed not self-claimed).
- **Gotcha — XP move keeps the original event payload:** an activity **category override** adjusts the LIFE `skill_progress` rows directly (`moveActivityXp`) and writes **no new `level_up_event`**; the original grant's ledger event still names the *old* skill. An accepted correction-history trade-off (documented in `ProgressionService.moveActivityXp`), not a bug.
- **Gotcha — uncategorized entries carry 0 XP until a pick:** a low-confidence (`< 0.6`) or classifier-off entry lands with `skill_key` null and `xp_awarded` 0; the stored `xp_suggested` (already clamped) is what a later manual categorization grants, within the day's remaining caps.
- **Gotcha — the caps make bad-faith logging economically irrelevant:** every activity is clamped to `[5,25]` XP and bounded by `per-skill-daily-cap` (40) and `daily-cap` (100). Spamming the log can't out-earn honest training, so the honor system needs no anti-cheat.
- **Gotcha — ACTIVITY quests don't auto-complete:** `evaluateAndFinalize` completes only DERIVED quests; an activity-mode GROWTH quest completes solely via `completeMatchingActivityQuest` on an activity write, and otherwise **expires quietly** with the day (no failure state).
- **Gotcha — robustness cannot carry quest XP** (E1, still true): the award tail recomputes `robustness` to an absolute streak target, wiping additive deltas — that is why FUELBIO quests route to the `recovery` LIFE skill.
- **Gotcha — flavor is cron-only (E3):** `QuestFlavor.rewrite` runs solely on `QuestJob.runGenerate`; the lazy `GET /api/quest/day/{date}` generation and rerolls **keep catalog copy** by design (re-flavoring copy the user has already seen would be jarring). Any rewrite failure — LLM error, unparseable JSON, count mismatch, blank/over-length entry — silently keeps the catalog copy: flavor is a garnish, never load-bearing.
- **Gotcha — savings counts only AI-extracted `financial` amounts (E3):** `savingsHuf30d` (and the growth-week savings) sum `extracted.amountHuf` **only over entries the classifier categorized to the `financial` LIFE skill** with a non-null extracted amount. A savings-like entry the AI mis-skilled, or one with no amount extracted, contributes nothing; a manual category override to/from `financial` shifts the LIFE XP row but does **not** re-extract an amount (the stored `extracted` envelope is set once at write). Deterministic and honest — no anti-cheat needed.
- **Gotcha — week attribution splits on `occurred_on` vs `occurred_at` (E3):** the growth-week `activities`/`savingsHuf` count by the entry's `occurred_on` date, while `lifeXp` sums `level_up_event.occurred_at` (the instant the XP was granted). A back-dated activity therefore lands in the past week's activity/savings numbers but in the current week's LIFE XP — inherent to the ledger design (mirrors the trait computation), not a bug.
- **Gotcha — adaptive reads only terminal statuses (E3):** the banding counts `completed`/`expired` closed rows in the trailing window; `offered`/`rerolled` never count, and difficulty always yields to availability then to the cooldown rule — so a sparse history simply falls back to all tiers, never an empty slot.
- **Gotcha — badges are derived on read, with NO unlock dates (`mezo-rmhr`):** the 9 achievement badges are computed each read by `AchievementService` from the live ledgers — deterministic, retroactive (a returning user's whole history counts), zero migration cost — so there is **no unlock ledger and no "unlocked on ⟨date⟩" label** (a deliberate deviation from the mockup's `✓ Júl 11` labels). A **persistent achievement table with unlock timestamps + a first-earn celebration overlay** is the recorded **future upgrade**; until then the badge only knows *achieved / current / target*, never *when*. Perks, by contrast, DO carry a real `unlockedAt` (they are persisted `perk_unlock` rows). A switch-off port makes its dependent badges honestly report `current=0`, not an error.
- **Gotcha — Össz XP is FE-summed (`mezo-rmhr`):** the hero "Össz XP" + the summary card's total chip sum `cumulativeXp` across all three bands **on the frontend** from the existing profile response — there is no backend total field. If a new band is added, the FE sum picks it up automatically; a backend total would need a new profile field.
