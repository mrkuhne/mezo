# Ritual — Napzárás Sleep-Anchored Daily Closing

> A full-screen, sleep-anchored **5-act evening closing ritual** at route `/ritual` (`AppLayout` child, tab bar hidden — the `train/session` precedent), entered from the Today evening daypart's **Napzárás CTA** (`DaypartEvening`, the `IslandEvening` successor — see [today.md](today.md)) or the evening habit chain. **Status: ✅ done** (backend R1+R2 + FE R3); `/ritual` visual goldens + the formal reduced-motion audit shipped in **R4** (`mezo-mzbz`).

## 1. Summary

**Napzárás** ("day-closing") turns the scattered end-of-day interactions — check-ins, the intention reflection, the activity journal, training, meals, sleep — into one anchored nightly habit: it recaps the day's story, closes what's left open, stages the day's XP/coin/skill harvest as a celebratory peak, and hands off into the existing sleep-prep evening routine. The whole evening is choreographed off the sleep goal's bed anchor: **day → Napzárás → sleep-prep (evening habit chain) → lights out.**

The design leans on ritual psychology (see the driving spec's §2 table): a fixed 5-act sequence for anxiety-reducing predictability, the **peak-end rule** (the celebratory Harvest act sits near the end, act 4 of 5, so a good ending retroactively colors the whole day), the **Zeigarnik effect** (act 3 exists solely to close open loops before sleep), visible progress as the strongest motivator (skill bars, XP count-up), and Fogg's "earned sparkle" principle (confetti only in Harvest, scaled to what actually happened). The tone is **ADR 0010** throughout: no punishment, no streak anxiety, a thin day gets warmth not fireworks, expired quests never render as failures.

Built in three epics under umbrella `mezo-vrq3`: **R1** `mezo-huzd` (the account gamification ledger — `feature/gamification`, the Harvest read `GET /api/gamification/day/{date}`, documented in [growth.md §2](growth.md)) and **R2** `mezo-hvmx` (the thin `feature/ritual` backend + the `evening_ritual` habit wiring, documented in [habit.md §5](habit.md)) shipped first and are consumed here, not re-documented; **R3** `mezo-ilsj` (this doc's primary subject) is the FE 5-act flow + Today's ritual entry (then `RitualCard`; since dissolved into the evening island, `mezo-euze`) + the `rz-*` animation family. **R4** `mezo-mzbz` added the `/ritual` visual goldens (Arrival act 1 + Harvest act 4, both themes, the usual darwin+linux two-platform sets) and the formal reduced-motion audit + guard test (§8, §9).

Status per layer: **backend** ✅ (`feature/ritual` — `ritual_day` table, `RitualService`, `RitualController`, switch-gated; the gamification ledger backing the Harvest read is a separate domain, [growth.md](growth.md)), **FE real** ✅ (the full 5-act flow + the evening island's Napzárás entry over the real endpoints), **FE mock** ✅ (deterministic seed — 22:30 bed anchor, window open, day not yet closed; close awards the mock HABIT XP once). Driving spec: [`2026-07-24-daily-closing-ritual-design.md`](../superpowers/specs/2026-07-24-daily-closing-ritual-design.md); ADR [0010](../decisions/0010-gamified-growth-xp-feedback-not-payment.md).

## 2. User-facing behavior

### Entry — the Today evening island's Napzárás CTA (`features/today/components/IslandEvening.tsx`, the 🌙 **Este** island's hero act slot — since the three-islands re-composition, `mezo-euze`, [ADR 0022](../decisions/0022-today-three-islands.md); `RitualCard` is deleted)

Three states derived from `useRitualDay` + the pure `ritualWindowState(now, window)` (`features/ritual/logic/ritualWindow.ts`) — the derivation is unchanged from the `RitualCard` era, only its rendering dissolved into the island's hero:

- **waiting** (before `window.opensAt`) — a quiet ghost button `Napzárás {opensAt}-kor nyílik` (`.td-ghost`, `DaypartEvening.tsx:122-126`) in the island's act row; the hero sub reads `napzárás {opensAt}-től · villanyoltás {bedTime}`. The ghost still navigates to `/ritual` — the window *nudges*, it never locks.
- **open** (from `opensAt` onward — a **soft, ~12h-wide** window, never re-closes itself before the next day) — the **lavender `Zárjuk le a napot` CTA** (`.td-cta.is-lav`, `DaypartEvening.tsx:117-121` — the app's one non-coral CTA) → `navigate('/ritual')`.
- **done** (the day is closed) — the CTA yields to a `Napzárás kész ✓` done line (`.td-foot`, `DaypartEvening.tsx:133`).

**The soft gate stays a nudge, not a lock** (ADR 0010, D2 of the spec): a direct `/ritual` visit works any time today, and since `mezo-euze` the waiting state's quiet ghost is again an in-app route into `/ritual` — the opening time on its label carries the "not yet" signal, so it is not a dead control (the `ItemRow` doctrine the `mezo-j7u4` no-CTA cut protected). **Still recorded:** the hero CTA is Today's single Napzárás surface — `IslandEvening` deliberately filters the `ritual:day` normalizer row out of its L1 (`OWNED_BY_RITUAL_HERO` + the `source !== 'ritual'` guard) because a row pill would be the weaker, non-window-aware duplicate. See [today.md §9](today.md).

The `?ritual=waiting|open|done` URL param (`useTodayScenario().ritual`) **wins over the derived state** — the `?day=` demo-affordance precedent, survives real mode by design for QA/demos.

### The evening habit row ([habit.md §2](habit.md))

The EVENING chain's 4th row, **`evening_ritual`** „Napzárás", is a DERIVED habit whose `Logolás` **navigates to `/ritual`** (`logic/habitAction.ts` maps `evening_ritual` to `{ kind: 'nav', to: '/ritual' }` — the same `nav` kind `morning_weigh_in`/`morning_coffee`/`morning_workout` already use, not a new `ritual`-specific kind). The habit completes derived once the ritual closes (§5). **The mapping is unchanged, but the row no longer renders on Today** (`mezo-j7u4`; filter relocated by `mezo-euze`): `IslandEvening.tsx`'s `OWNED_BY_RITUAL_HERO` filters it out of the evening L1, since the island's Napzárás hero CTA already owns the same act with a stronger affordance. It is still rendered by Growth's „Rutin" tab, so the second entry point moved rather than disappearing — on Today the hero is now the single one.

### The 5 acts (`RitualPage.tsx`, state machine over `act: 1..5`, progress dots `● ● ○ ○ ○`, ✕ exits to `/today` anytime)

1. **Megérkezés** (`ArrivalStep.tsx`) — night-sky takeover, a breathing moon, the fixed lines „A nap véget ért." / „Zárjuk le együtt." (byte-exact per the approved mockup — do not paraphrase), `Kezdjük 🌙` CTA into act 2. No data.
2. **A napod íve** (`DayStoryStep.tsx`) — the `DayArc` Bézier reprised large (`buildArcPoints`/`pointXY` reused from `features/today/logic/dayArc.ts`, `workoutTime: null` — the workout gets its own recap row instead of a second dot), the arc's check-in dots pop in staggered, then the day's recap event rows from `useDayRecap` (§3). On a thin day (< 2 done recap events **and** < 2 check-ins) a soft acceptance line renders — „Ma ennyi fért bele. Az is számít." — **never** a gap/failure list (ADR 0010).
3. **Nyitott hurkok** (`LoopsStep.tsx`, the only interactive act) — closes the day's two **gated** open loops via `openLoops()` (§3): a missing check-in slot (`Koppints` → signals `onOpenCheckIn`, the page mounts `CheckInSheet`) and, only when the day has ≥ 1 intention focus, the evening reflection (inline `Igen`/`Részben`/`Nem` buttons calling `useIntentionActions(date).reflect` directly — no sheet). When neither is open: „Minden hurok zárva ✓". A **third, evergreen** row — „Történt még valami ma?" → `Napló` — always renders, is never glowing, and never participates in the "nothing open" check (you can always log one more thing). `Tovább` always advances regardless of loop state — nothing here is mandatory.
4. **Termés** (`HarvestStep.tsx`, the peak) — on **entering** this act, `RitualPage` fires `useRitualActions(date).close(ringsOf(states))` exactly once (a `closedRef` guard) — **since `mezo-dhzk`** the call also carries the day's six Életjel-ring values (`useNeeds` snapshotted once at page mount, `ringsOf` from `features/today/logic/needsInputs.ts`), so the same close that ends the day also stores the needs day-close snapshot and awards its bonus XP (see [needs.md §5](needs.md#5-integrations)); the habit read then completes `evening_ritual` (+10 XP). The stage shows: a `CountUp` XP total, per-source chips (`GET /api/gamification/day/{date}`'s `xpBySource`, HU-labelled QUEST/HABIT/ACTIVITY/GYM/RUN/SPORT — 5 other wire members MEAL/WEIGHT/SLEEP/CHECKIN/MEDICATION are defensively skipped, not rendered unlabelled), coin chips (`coinEvents`), an optional LIFE skill-bar highlight (the skill with the highest within-level progress that hasn't maxed out — see §9 for why the "N XP to next level" hint is dropped), the streak flame (dim + "— megszakadt" when `!streakAlive`, the `AppHero` dead-streak-copy precedent), and — **new, `mezo-dhzk`** — a second "🛟 N napja életben" line from `useNeedsSummary()` whenever the needs streak is > 0 (the two streaks are independent: this one tracks all-six-rings-green closes, not the habit/ritual streak above it). Confetti (10 particles, transform-only) bursts around the streak beat only when `xpTotal > 0` — a thin day gets no confetti. The reward block is **vertically centered** (the `rz-harvest-body` wrapper, `margin-block: auto`) with the `Tovább` CTA pinned to the bottom (`mezo-tr5v`) — no top-loaded empty space; `Tovább` advances to act 5.
5. **Elengedés** (`ReleaseStep.tsx`) — the arc closes into a full circle around the moon, the fixed closing line „A nap le van zárva. Elengedheted. 🌙", the companion's „napzárás" closing note when present (honest absence otherwise), and a **handoff panel** — „MOST JÖN · ALVÁS-ELŐKÉSZÍTÉS" listing the two remaining stops (lecsendesítés at `prepStartsAt`, villanyoltás at `bedTime` — straight from the ritual window, no new data) + `Esti rutin indítása →`, which navigates to `/today` (the 🌙 **Este** island owns the sleep-prep phase from there — its `windDown`-phase hero content, incl. the winddown `Leállás megvolt ✓` ghost, plus the evening habit chain's L1 `ItemRow`s (`IslandList`, since `mezo-euze`) — integration, not duplication; this component never renders prep-step UI itself).

**Nothing is written before act 4.** Exiting (✕) at any point up to entering act 4 is consequence-free — no close call fires.

## 3. Architecture & data flow

```
IslandEvening (Este island hero)      RitualPage (act state machine, /ritual)
  → useRitualDay(date)                  → useRitualDay(date)              — window + closed state
      + ritualWindowState(now, window)  → useDayRecap(date)               — act 2 (pure composition, ZERO new fetches)
                                         → useCheckins() / useIntentionDay(date)+useIntentionActions
                                                                            — act 3 (reused Today hooks/sheets)
                                         → useNeeds(tickNow)               — snapshotted once at mount (mezo-dhzk)
                                         → useRitualActions(date).close(ringsOf(states))
                                                                            — fires ONCE on entering act 4
                                         → useGamificationDay(date) + useProgressionProfile()
                                             + useNeedsSummary()           — act 4 (the Harvest read)
                                         → useCompanionNote()              — act 5 closing note
      mock: ritualMock.ts (mockRitualDay/EMPTY_RITUAL_DAY)
      real: ritualApi.ts → GET /api/ritual/day/{date} | POST /api/ritual/close
              → RitualController → RitualService (getDay/close)
              → SleepAnchorPort.resolve(userId)   — the window's bed anchor
              → RitualDayRepository               — the ritual_day existence row
```

- **`useRitualDay`** (`data/ritual/ritualHooks.ts`) is a **`useDualQuery`**: mock returns `mockRitualDay(date)` (a fixed 22:30 bed anchor, window open, `closed: false`) synchronously; real returns the honest-empty `EMPTY_RITUAL_DAY(date)` (ghost window mirroring the backend's no-sleep-goal config default, bed 22:00) while unresolved — never the seed. Query key `['ritualDay', date]`.
- **`useRitualActions(date).close(rings?)`** — **since `mezo-dhzk`** `close` takes an optional `NeedsRingsWire` (the day's Életjel-ring snapshot, `ringsOf(useNeeds(...).states)`; `RitualPage` always passes it, so in practice the param is only optional for other/older callers and tests). Mock: patches the cache to `closed: true` idempotently (a second call on an already-closed day is a no-op — "no second award"), ticks the DERIVED `evening_ritual` chain row via `completeMockDerivedHabit(qc, date, 'evening_ritual')` (`@/data/habit/habitHooks`, `mezo-o5hx` — the mock mirror of the server-side `ritual_closed` derivation), calls `awardGamificationEvent(qc, { type: 'HABIT', xpOverride: 10 })` (the intention first-focus precedent), and — only inside the not-yet-closed branch, after the ritual's own award — calls `applyMockNeedsClose(qc, date, rings)` when `rings` is present (`data/needs/needsHooks.ts`; it carries its own `lastCloseDate` idempotency guard too, belt-and-braces against a re-close double-award). Real: POSTs `/api/ritual/close`, then — **best-effort, non-blocking** — `needsApi.close(date, rings)` when `rings` is present (a `try/catch` swallow: a needs-service hiccup must never block the napzárás itself), then invalidates **six** query keys in an **ordered, awaited** sequence, not a concurrent fan-out (`mezo-ywz1`): first `await invalidateQueries(['habitDay', date])` alone — this is the derived-completion refetch (see below), and `RitualPage` mounting `useHabitDay(date)` is what gives it an active observer to actually refetch (not just mark-stale); only once that settles does `['gamificationDay', date]` + `['gamification']` + `['progressionProfile']` invalidate together (`Promise.all`, since the +10 they need to read now exists); `['dailyQuests', date]`, `['ritualDay', date]`, and `['needsSummary']` invalidate last, fire-and-forget (not harvest-critical). The mock path is unaffected by this ordering — it's a synchronous cache patch, no invalidation race is possible. Full needs-award rule (per-ring + all-green bonus + streak) documented in [needs.md §4](needs.md#4-data-model--api); this doc covers only how the ritual close **carries** the snapshot.
- **`useDayRecap(date)`** (`data/ritual/recapHooks.ts`) is the act-2 "day story" — a **pure composition over already-fetched domain hooks** (`useCheckins`, `useActivities`, `useIntentionDay`, `useFuelDay`, `useTrain`, `useSleep`, `useWeight`, `useCompanionNote`), **zero new fetches**, mirroring the `useToday` composition precedent (sanctioned cross-domain hook composition in the data layer, not a new backend read). It derives `{ events, checkinsDone, thinDay, closingNote }`: training (real done-state from `completedTodayWorkout`, mock **always** `done: false` — no fabricated completion signal) or, on a rest day, a `today`-flagged volleyball session; a single meals-aggregate event (protein sum); weight **only if today has a logged entry** (no stale-row fallback); sleep from the date-less always-latest `useSleep`; one event per activity-log entry; one event per intention focus (its `done` mirrors the day's single holistic reflection, not per-focus state). Supplements are **deliberately skipped** — `useFuelDay().fuel.supplements` is the static demo list in **both** modes (never swapped for a real read), so deriving an event from it would fabricate data in real mode (§9). `thinDay` = fewer than 2 done recap events **and** fewer than 2 check-ins; `closingNote` surfaces only a companion note of `kind === 'closing'`.
- **`openLoops({ checkins, intention })`** (`features/ritual/logic/openLoops.ts`, pure) derives `{ checkinOpen, reflectOpen }` — `checkinOpen` reuses the TodayPage next-open-slot predicate; `reflectOpen` is `foci.length > 0 && reflection == null` (no focus at all ⇒ never open, nothing to reflect on). The journal invite is a deliberate third row **outside** this shape — evergreen, never "closes".
- **`ritualWindowState(now, window)`** (`features/ritual/logic/ritualWindow.ts`, pure) — **wrap-safe**: everything is computed as minutes-forward-from-`opensAt` (never lexical HH:mm comparison), so a bed time past midnight (e.g. `opensAt: 23:15`) still derives correctly. `'open'` = within the 12h window forward of `opensAt`; a companion `minutesUntil(now, hhmm)` helper is shared by both.
- **`harvestStages({ sources, coins, hasSkillHighlight })`** (`features/ritual/logic/harvestStages.ts`, pure) — the act-4 reward-choreography **schedule only** (no timers/animation itself): fixed cadence `xp-total` at 400ms → each source chip +250ms → the coin run starting 300ms after the last pre-coin beat, +250ms per coin → an optional skill highlight +400ms after → the streak flame always closing the sequence +400ms after whatever came before. `HarvestStep` staggers its `CountUp`/pop-in `animationDelay`s directly off this schedule.
- **Habit read-triggered close-evaluation:** `HabitEvaluator`'s `ritual_closed` metric (`INTRADAY_METRICS`) re-checks `RitualDayRepository` on every today-read, so the EVENING chain's `evening_ritual` row flips `pending → done` on the very next `useHabitDay` read after close — no polling, the read is the trigger (the `useDailyQuests`/`useHabitDay` `staleTime: 0` precedent, see [habit.md §3](habit.md)). `RitualPage` mounts `useHabitDay(date)` itself (an otherwise-unused-return mount, `staleTime: 0` in real mode) purely to give `['habitDay', date]` an active observer **during the flow**, so that read fires as part of `close()`'s invalidation instead of waiting for the next `/today` visit (`mezo-ywz1`).
- **Level-up suppression:** entering act 4 fires `close()` then `useHabitActions(date).consumeLevelUps()` — the Harvest stage IS the celebration for the ritual's own habit completion, so any habit levelUp sitting in the cache is silently dropped rather than also firing the global `LevelUpScreen` overlay a second time once the user lands back on `/today`. **Covered by the FE test suite (MSW-mocked real mode) in both modes, and verified live on the real Postgres stack (R4, `mezo-ywz1`, 2026-07-25): after `close()` the Harvest advanced 0 → 10 XP with the ritual's own `☀️ Rutin +10`, and returning to `/today` flashed no second `LevelUpScreen` (that run's +10 did not cross a level threshold, so the suppression branch itself remains exercised by the FE suite's level-up scenario).** In REAL mode this depends on the `mezo-ywz1` fix above: `RitualPage`'s `useHabitDay(date)` mount gives `close()`'s `['habitDay', date]` invalidation an observer to refetch, and `close()` **awaits** that refetch before resolving, so by the time `.then(() => consumeLevelUps())` runs, the ritual's own level-up is already sitting in the cache to be dropped — not a stale cache from before the close.

## 4. Data model & API

**Table `ritual_day`** (migration [`202607241000_mezo-hvmx_create_ritual_day.sql`](../../backend/src/main/resources/db/changelog/1.0.0/script/202607241000_mezo-hvmx_create_ritual_day.sql), the `intention_creed`-style existence-record pattern): `id uuid pk`, `created_by`, soft-delete cols, `ritual_date date`, `closed_at timestamptz`. Partial unique `uq_ritual_day_user_date (created_by, ritual_date) where is_deleted = false`. In the `ResetDatabase` TRUNCATE list (`support/ResetDatabase.java:40`).

**`RitualService`** (`feature/ritual/service/RitualService.java`, gated `mezo.feature.ritual.enabled`): `getDay(userId, date)` — pure read, **never 404** for a valid date, composes `{date, closed, closedAt?, window}` where `window` (`opensAt`/`prepStartsAt`/`bedTime`) derives fresh from `SleepAnchorPort.resolve(userId).bed()` on every call (`bed − leadMin` / `bed − prepLeadMin` / `bed`, the `HabitTargets` idiom — ghosts the port's config default when no sleep goal exists). `close(userId, date)` — **today only** (else 409 `RITUAL_NOT_TODAY`); **idempotent** (an already-closed day's `findByCreatedByAndRitualDate` short-circuits the insert, same response); a `DataIntegrityViolationException` catch re-reads the winner's row on a concurrent-close race (the `HabitService.ensureRows` precedent). **Awards nothing itself** — completion XP rides the habit's own `HABIT` tail (§5).

**Contract** ([`api/feature/ritual/ritual.yml`](../../api/feature/ritual/ritual.yml), tag `Ritual` → `RitualApi`, `RitualController implements RitualApi`, gated `mezo.feature.ritual.enabled` — off ⇒ 404, no ritual beans):

| Method + path | Operation | Returns | Errors |
|---|---|---|---|
| `GET /api/ritual/day/{date}` | `getRitualDay` | `RitualDayResponse{date, closed, closedAt?, window{opensAt, prepStartsAt, bedTime}}` — never 404 | 401 |
| `POST /api/ritual/close` (`{date}`) | `closeRitualDay` | `RitualDayResponse` (same response on repeat close) | 409 `RITUAL_NOT_TODAY` |

**Config:** switch `mezo.feature.ritual.enabled` (`FeaturesConfiguration.RITUAL_SWITCH`); `RitualProperties` (`@Validated`, prefix `mezo.ritual`): `lead-min` 75 (`15..240`), `prep-lead-min` 45 (`0..180`) — never code constants.

**FE types** (`data/types.ts:911-913`): `Reflection = 'yes'|'partial'|'no'` (shared with intention), `RitualWindow { opensAt, prepStartsAt, bedTime }`, `RitualDay { date, closed, closedAt, window }`. Wire↔domain in `data/ritual/ritualApi.ts` (`toDay`); mock seed in `data/ritual/ritualMock.ts` (`mockRitualDay`/`EMPTY_RITUAL_DAY`).

**The Harvest read is a separate domain's contract** — `GET /api/gamification/day/{date}` (`GamificationDayResponse{xpBySource[], xpTotal, coinEvents[], coinTotal, streakDays, streakAlive}`), owned by `feature/gamification` (R1, `mezo-huzd`) and documented in [growth.md §2](growth.md)/[§4](growth.md); this doc only documents how the ritual **consumes** it (§5).

**The `evening_ritual` habit + its `ritual_closed` metric** live in the **habit** catalog/evaluator (`content/habit-catalog.json`, `HabitEvaluator`), documented in [habit.md §4](habit.md)/[§5](habit.md) — not restated here; this doc documents only the `RitualDayRepository` read they perform.

## 5. Integrations

All backend dependencies are **pure reads** — ritual depends on {sleep goal}; nothing backend-side depends back on ritual **except** habit's evaluator read (a one-way pure read, not a cycle).

- **← Sleep goal** (`RitualService`, via `SleepAnchorPort`): the window's `bedTime`/`opensAt`/`prepStartsAt` derive fresh from the shared sleep-anchor port (the `HabitTargets` idiom) — no sleep-goal-side change, ghosts the port's config default (bed 22:00) when no sleep goal exists.
- **↔ Habit** ([habit.md §5](habit.md)): **ritual → habit is one-way** — `HabitEvaluator`'s `ritual_closed` metric reads `RitualDayRepository` directly (a plain JPA bean, the intention-repos precedent); the ritual domain never reads habit. The FE side mirrors this: the EVENING chain's `evening_ritual` row keeps its `/ritual` `nav` mapping (`habitAction.ts`), and closing the ritual invalidates `['habitDay', date]` so it flips to `done` on the next read — visible on Growth's Rutin tab and in the Este face's chain progress, though the row itself is filtered off Today (§2). The habit's sleep-prep siblings (`wind_down`, `bed_on_time`) are **not** part of the ritual — they come after it (D3 of the spec).
- **← Intention, Check-in, Activity** (act 3, `LoopsStep`): reuses `useIntentionActions(date).reflect` ([intention.md](intention.md)), `useCheckins().saveCheckIn` + the shared `CheckInSheet` ([today.md §2](today.md)), and `ActivityLogSheet` ([growth.md §2](growth.md)) — **one write path per signal**, mounted at the `RitualPage` level exactly like `TodayPage` mounts `CheckInSheet`. No new write surface is introduced; the ritual only signals which sheet to open.
- **← Proactive** (act 5, `ReleaseStep`): renders the existing 20:30 heartbeat "napzárás" companion note via `useCompanionNote()` when present, honest absence otherwise ([proactive.md](proactive.md)) — no proactive-side change.
- **→ Progression:** the close's own XP rides the existing **HABIT** award tail (`evening_ritual`'s catalog XP) — **no new progression source type**. The Harvest stage reads `level_up_event`-derived aggregates (via the gamification day endpoint) + `useProgressionProfile()` for the skill-bar highlight; it introduces no new progression contract.
- **→ Gamification** ([growth.md §2](growth.md)): the ritual's Harvest stage (`HarvestStep`) is the **primary UI consumer** of `GET /api/gamification/day/{date}` via **`useGamificationDay(date)`** — the hook existed since R1 with no UI consumer; R3 is what wires it up. It reads the day's own `xpBySource`/`coinEvents`/`streakDays`/`streakAlive` snapshot deliberately (not `useGamification()`'s account-level read) — the two mocks intentionally diverge (day seed vs. account-profile seed) since the Harvest act reports *tonight's* numbers, not a stale account figure. **In REAL mode**, the Harvest total/`☀️ Rutin` chip now includes the ritual's own +10 `evening_ritual` closure XP (`mezo-ywz1`): `close()` awaits the `['habitDay', date]` refetch (which `RitualPage`'s `useHabitDay(date)` mount makes actually happen) before invalidating `['gamificationDay', date]`, so the derived award is persisted before the Harvest re-read fires. Covered by the FE test suite (MSW-mocked real mode) in both modes, and verified live on the real Postgres stack (R4, `mezo-ywz1`, 2026-07-25): closing the ritual on a clean `demodata` account moved the Harvest total 0 → 10 and rendered the `☀️ Rutin +10` chip (the streak also advanced 6 → 7), confirming the awaited `habitDay` → `gamificationDay` sequence lands the derived +10 before the re-read.
- **→ Today:** since `mezo-j7u4` Today has a **single Napzárás entry point** — since `mezo-euze` that is the 🌙 Este island's hero CTA (`IslandEvening`, §2; formerly `RitualCard`); the `evening_ritual` row is filtered off Today in its favour, and the `ritual:day` normalizer row with it. `useDayRecap` only **reads** existing Today-adjacent hooks, adds nothing to `useToday`. **Today's own day model now shares this domain's clock:** `features/today/logic/dayFace.ts` derives its three daypart windows from the same `useSleepGoal()` wake/bed anchor `ritualWindow.ts` uses, so the Este island and the Napzárás window can never disagree about when evening is. Today also still hosts the arc: `DayStoryStep` imports `buildArcPoints`/`pointXY` from `features/today/logic/dayArc.ts`, whose own component was deleted — see [today.md §9](today.md).
- **↔ Theme (design system, `mezo-tr5v`):** the `rz-screen` is a hard-dark takeover regardless of theme, but the sheets it portals (`CheckInSheet`, `ActivityLogSheet`) and any XP-award overlay (`LevelUpScreen`) are theme-aware and would render **light** for a light-mode user — clashing. `RitualPage` therefore calls `useTheme().setForceTheme('dark')` on mount and `setForceTheme(null)` on unmount (a transient override that does **not** touch the persisted `mode`), so the whole flow — including every portaled surface — renders dark, then reverts to the user's real theme on exit. See [`_platform-design-system.md` §5](_platform-design-system.md) (theme cascade).
- **↔ Account progression / `AppHero` (mock-mode side-effect):** the mock close call is one more call site of the mock account-XP precedent ([growth.md §2](growth.md)) — real mode never calls it (account XP is derived from the profile).

## 6. How to use it (consume)

```ts
import { useRitualDay, useRitualActions, useDayRecap } from '@/data/hooks'

const { data: ritualDay } = useRitualDay(date)          // RitualDay — { closed, window }
const { close, pending } = useRitualActions(date)
await close()                                            // idempotent; real mode invalidates 6 keys
await close(ringsOf(needs.states))                       // + best-effort needs day-close snapshot (mezo-dhzk)

const { events, checkinsDone, thinDay, closingNote } = useDayRecap(date)  // pure, zero new fetches
```

```ts
import { ritualWindowState } from '@/features/ritual/logic/ritualWindow'
const state = ritualWindowState(new Date(), ritualDay.window)  // 'waiting' | 'open'
```

- Never import `ritualApi`/`ritualMock` directly — go through `@/data/hooks`.
- `useRitualDay` never 404s and has no ghost-guard obligation — the window is always present (config-ghosted absent a sleep goal). `IslandEvening` still branches on `ritualDay.closed` for its `Napzárás kész ✓` done state.
- Query keys: `['ritualDay', date]`; a real close write invalidates `['ritualDay', date]` + `['habitDay', date]` + `['dailyQuests', date]` + `['gamificationDay', date]` + `['gamification']` + `['progressionProfile']` + (since `mezo-dhzk`) `['needsSummary']`.
- `useDayRecap` composes several other domains' hooks — do not call it more than once per screen if avoidable (each call re-runs the same composition; TanStack Query dedupes the underlying fetches by key, so this is a CPU/readability concern, not a network one).

## 7. How to extend it

- **Add a 6th act:** insert a new `act` branch in `RitualPage.tsx`'s render switch, bump `ACT_COUNT`, and place the new component under `features/ritual/components/`. Nothing writes before the Harvest act (act 4) by convention — keep new pre-Harvest acts read-only/consequence-free on ✕ exit; a new **post**-Harvest act should not re-fire `close()`.
- **Add a recap event (act 2):** extend `useDayRecap` (`data/ritual/recapHooks.ts`) with a new `RecapEvent` push — **pure reads only** over an existing hook (the `own_recipe_meal`/`intention_focus_set` precedent from habit/quest), never a new fetch. If the source data isn't real in both modes yet (the fuel-supplements gotcha, §9), report honest absence rather than fabricate a `done` event.
- **Add a Harvest source label:** `HarvestStep.tsx`'s `SOURCE_LABEL_HU` is a closed `Record<KnownSource, string>` gated by `isKnownSource` — a new `XpEventType` source (backend `level_up_event.source_type`) needs a matching HU label + `CHIP_ICON_BY_SOURCE` entry (`features/progression/logic/levelUpMeta.ts`) added here, or it is defensively skipped (never rendered unlabelled).
- **Tune the window:** `RitualProperties` (`mezo.ritual.lead-min`/`mezo.ritual.prep-lead-min`), never a code constant ([`configuration_conventions.md`](../references/configuration_conventions.md)).
- **Tune the Harvest choreography timing:** edit the constants inline in `harvestStages.ts` (delay deltas) — it is pure and unit-tested (`harvestStages.test.ts`), so a timing change is a one-file, fully-covered edit.
- **DS re-dress (`mezo-setx.5.3`, 2026-08-05):** the whole `rz-*` family speaks the Mezo-edition DS tokens — the takeover surface is the Pulse dark ramp (`--surface-elevated/card/page` radial, valid because `RitualPage` forces `data-theme=dark`), the cream washes are `color-mix` off `--text-primary`, lavender rides `--dv-lav` + the kept `--lav-deep` carrier (spec D10 — the ritual's settled night direction, deliberately NOT the primary ramp even on CTAs), done-marks the success ramp, the streak `--primary-base`, coins/confetti the `--dv-amber`/data-viz band (`CONFETTI_COLOR` in `HarvestStep` moved to `--dv-*` tokens; `ReleaseStep`'s moon fill is token-mixed). Type roles: the Harvest total is the DS hero numeral (56 / Geist 200 / −0.04em), the release line the coach voice (22/200), the thin-day line + closing note + loop beat the Fraunces meta voice (14/500i), body copy 14, eyebrows 11/700/0.18em; radii/z snapped to the scales; the ✕ exit grew a 44px hit area and every CTA a 48px min-height. Behaviour, act machine, close-ordering and the reduced-motion block are untouched (`reducedMotionGuard.test.ts` still passes unchanged); all four `ritual-*` goldens regenerated.
- **New animation:** extend the `rz-*` CSS family (`prototype.css`, tail of the file) rather than introducing a new family — it already reuses/extends the Napív `np-draw`/`np-pop`/`np-twinkle`/`pulse-soft` keyframes plus the ritual-local `fall` (confetti) and `glowp` (CTA pulse) keyframes. Every new loop/entrance MUST land behind the existing `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` block at the CSS tail (or the shared `useReducedMotion` hook for JS-driven animation, the `CountUp` precedent) — component tests stub `matchMedia` to reduced (the `LevelUpScreen.test.tsx` pattern).
- House standards: contract-first ([`api_contract_conventions.md`](../references/api_contract_conventions.md)), backend per [`docs/references/*.md`](../references/), dual-mode hook recipe in [`_platform-data-layer.md`](_platform-data-layer.md).

## 9. Decisions, gotchas & deferred

- **Decisions (spec §3 D1–D10):** full-screen 5-act flow, `AppLayout` child with hidden tab bar (D1); sleep-anchored soft gateway — the window nudges, never locks (D2); the ritual is the evening home, **composing, not duplicating** existing surfaces (D3); small closure XP through the existing HABIT tail, no new progression source (D4); two backend slices — R1 gamification ledger + R2 thin ritual domain (D5); no duplicate XP table — `level_up_event` IS the ledger (D6); read-time streak + idempotent coin awards (D7); CSS-first animation, no new dependency (D8); two entry points — the Today ritual entry + the habit row (D9; **the Today half was narrowed by [ADR 0014](../decisions/0014-today-daypart-faces.md) and re-homed by [ADR 0022](../decisions/0022-today-three-islands.md)** — the evening island's hero CTA is Today's only entry point, the habit row survives on Growth's Rutin tab, §2); warm-night + lavender visual direction (D10). Tone: ADR [0010](../decisions/0010-gamified-growth-xp-feedback-not-payment.md).
- **Gotcha — supplements are skipped from the day recap.** `useFuelDay().fuel.supplements` is the static demo list from `data/fuel/fuel.ts` in **both** modes (never swapped for a real read anywhere in `fuelHooks.ts`) — rendering a "supplements n/m" recap beat from it would show fabricated data in real mode, so the honest-absence rule wins over the design spec's aspirational beat (§4 of the spec lists it; it was deliberately dropped in implementation).
- **Gotcha — the skill "N XP to next level" hint is dropped.** The spec's Harvest copy imagines "még N XP a Lv M-ig"; the wire `SkillLevel` type only carries `level` + `progressPct` (the within-level fill), no per-skill XP-to-next-level curve (`gamification/levelCurve.ts`'s `xpToNext` is the **account**-level curve, not per-skill, and no other FE surface derives this figure either). Fabricating N from `progressPct` alone would require an assumed XP-per-level the FE cannot honestly know, so `HarvestStep` renders only the bar + `Lv N`.
- **Gotcha — the title-unlock beat is deferred.** The spec's act-4 table mentions "title unlock if due"; no title-unlock detection or celebration exists in `HarvestStep` — out of scope for R3, not wired to any hook yet.
- **Gotcha — the Harvest streak reads the DAY snapshot, not the account snapshot.** `HarvestStep` deliberately uses `useGamificationDay(date)`'s own `streakDays`/`streakAlive`, not `useGamification()`'s account-level read — the two mock seeds intentionally diverge (day: 12/alive vs. account: 6/alive) so a future consumer doesn't accidentally conflate them.
- **Gotcha — `habitAction.ts` has no dedicated `ritual` kind.** The spec's §7 language ("a new `ritual` kind") is realized as the existing generic `nav` kind with `to: '/ritual'` — no new `HabitAction` variant was needed since `nav` already generalizes "go to this route."
- **Gotcha — level-ups are suppressed on ritual close, covered by the FE test suite in both modes and verified live (R4, `mezo-ywz1`).** Entering act 4 calls `consumeLevelUps()` right after `close()` so Today's habit level-up effect (`TodayPage.tsx:136-141`, formerly `RoutineCard`'s) never fires the global `LevelUpScreen` overlay a second time back on `/today` — the Harvest stage IS that celebration. Real mode depends on `RitualPage` mounting `useHabitDay(date)` (giving `['habitDay', date]` an active observer) and `close()` awaiting that key's invalidation before resolving (`mezo-ywz1`) — without the observer, the invalidation is a no-op refetch-wise and `consumeLevelUps()` runs too early. This ordering is exercised by the MSW-mocked FE test suite in both modes, and was verified live on the real Postgres stack (2026-07-25): closing the ritual advanced the Harvest to the +10 `☀️ Rutin` and returning to `/today` showed no second `LevelUpScreen` (`RitualCard` flipped to its done state, the EVENING chain to 1/6 · +10 XP). That run predates the daypart faces; the suppression mechanism is unchanged (the effect moved from `RoutineCard` to `TodayPage`, same consume-once call), but note the observed surfaces are now the Este island's Napzárás done line + the L1 chain rows (`mezo-euze`). This run's +10 did not cross a level threshold, so the suppression branch itself stays covered by the FE suite's level-up scenario. A future act that also grants XP (if ever added) must respect this suppression, not fight it.
- **Journal invite is evergreen by design** (`openLoops.ts`'s doc comment) — gating it on "already logged today" would make it permanently vanish in mock mode (the mock `useActivities` seed is date-invariant) and contradicts its "always askable" nature, unlike the two scheduled loops.
- **Gotcha — real-mode close→habit→harvest ordering, fixed (`mezo-ywz1`).** Originally no component on `/ritual` observed `['habitDay', date]`, so `close()`'s invalidation of that key was a no-op refetch-wise in real mode: the ritual's own +10 `evening_ritual` XP and its level-up only landed in the cache on the next `GET /api/habit/day` (i.e. after returning to `/today`), not during the flow. Fixed by (1) `RitualPage` mounting `useHabitDay(date)` purely for its observer side effect, and (2) `useRitualActions(date).close()`'s real path awaiting `['habitDay', date]`'s invalidation **before** invalidating `['gamificationDay', date]`/`['gamification']`/`['progressionProfile']`, instead of firing all six invalidations concurrently. Any future ritual-adjacent derived-completion-on-read pattern must follow the same observer-mount + ordered-await shape, not assume `invalidateQueries` alone guarantees a refetch.
- **R4 (`mezo-mzbz`) — done:** the `/ritual` visual goldens (Arrival act 1 + Harvest act 4, frozen clock, both themes, darwin+linux — `tests/visual/visual.spec.ts`) and the formal reduced-motion audit landed. **Audit result:** every active animation on a ritual-family selector is neutralized under `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` — 10 via an explicit `animation: none` in the `rz-*` tail reduce block (`.rz-moon`/`.rz-cta`/`.rz-ring`/`.rz-circle-moon`/`.rz-handoff-cta`/`.rz-arc-path`/`.rz-arc-dot`/`.rz-pop`/`.rz-skill-bar i`/`.np-anim`), and the `.rz-conf` confetti children via the container's `display: none`. (The 11th at audit time was `.ritcard-moon`'s breathing, guarded by a `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference` opt-in — **that rule no longer exists**: the `.ritcard*` family was deleted in `mezo-j7u4`. The guard test still scans for `.ritcard` selectors, so it stays correct-by-vacuity for that family and would catch one if it ever came back; Today's own replacement motion family — since `mezo-euze` the island families (`isl-morph`/`isl-floaty`/`isl-rowin`/`isl-phasein`, the retired `.faceswap`'s successors) — has its own guard, `features/today/todayReducedMotion.test.ts`, [today.md §8](today.md).) — enforced going forward by `features/ritual/reducedMotionGuard.test.ts` (§8), which fails on any newly-introduced unguarded ritual animation. The `CountUp` reduced-motion/jsdom immediate-value path is covered by `CountUp.test.tsx`. **Out of scope (spec §11):** PWA push/notification reminder at window opening; hard-gating sleep-prep behind the ritual; a "day grade" self-rating (the intention reflection + 20:00 check-in already carry the evaluative signal); weekly/monthly recap acts (Insights Weekly + Memoir own that horizon); companion-generated dynamic copy beyond the existing heartbeat note; retroactively closing a past day (a missed ritual lapses quietly, ADR 0010).
