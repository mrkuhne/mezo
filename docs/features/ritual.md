# Ritual — Napzárás Sleep-Anchored Daily Closing

> A full-screen, sleep-anchored **6-act evening closing ritual** at route `/ritual` (`AppLayout` child, chrome hidden — the `train/session` precedent: the `hideChrome` list suppresses the shell `AppHeader`, the `TabBar` and the `QuickLogFab` alike), entered from the Nap hub's Este panel **Napzárás CTA** (`NapHubPage`, the `DaypartEvening` successor since mezo-d20.2.1 — see [today.md](today.md)) or the evening habit chain. **Status: ✅ done** (backend R1+R2 + FE R3); `/ritual` visual goldens + the formal reduced-motion audit shipped in **R4** (`mezo-mzbz`).

## 1. Summary

**Napzárás** ("day-closing") turns the scattered end-of-day interactions — check-ins, the intention reflection, the activity journal, training, meals, sleep — into one anchored nightly habit: it recaps the day's story, closes what's left open, stages the day's XP/coin/skill harvest as a celebratory peak, and hands off into the existing sleep-prep evening routine. The whole evening is choreographed off the sleep goal's bed anchor: **day → Napzárás → sleep-prep (evening habit chain) → lights out.**

The design leans on ritual psychology (see the driving spec's §2 table): a fixed act sequence for anxiety-reducing predictability, the **peak-end rule** (the celebratory Harvest act sits near the end — act 5 of 6 since `mezo-b3pp.2`, act 4 of 5 before it — so a good ending retroactively colors the whole day), the **Zeigarnik effect** (act 4 exists solely to close open loops before sleep), visible progress as the strongest motivator (skill bars, XP count-up), and Fogg's "earned sparkle" principle (confetti only in Harvest, scaled to what actually happened). The tone is **ADR 0010** throughout: no punishment, no streak anxiety, a thin day gets warmth not fireworks, expired quests never render as failures.

Built in three epics under umbrella `mezo-vrq3`: **R1** `mezo-huzd` (the account gamification ledger — `feature/gamification`, the Harvest read `GET /api/gamification/day/{date}`, documented in [growth.md §2](growth.md)) and **R2** `mezo-hvmx` (the thin `feature/ritual` backend + the `evening_ritual` habit wiring, documented in [habit.md §5](habit.md)) shipped first and are consumed here, not re-documented; **R3** `mezo-ilsj` (this doc's primary subject) is the FE 5-act flow + Today's ritual entry (then `RitualCard`; since dissolved into the evening island, `mezo-euze`) + the `rz-*` animation family. **R4** `mezo-mzbz` added the `/ritual` visual goldens (Arrival + Harvest, both themes, the usual darwin+linux two-platform sets) and the formal reduced-motion audit + guard test (§8, §9). **Phase 5 W1.2** (`mezo-b3pp.2`, spec §5.2) added the **sixth act** — the optional prose reflection at act 3 — which is also what made `closed_at is not null`, not row existence, the day's closure predicate (§4).

Status per layer: **backend** ✅ (`feature/ritual` — `ritual_day` table, `RitualService`, `RitualController`, switch-gated; the gamification ledger backing the Harvest read is a separate domain, [growth.md](growth.md)), **FE real** ✅ (the full 6-act flow + the evening island's Napzárás entry over the real endpoints), **FE mock** ✅ (deterministic seed — 22:30 bed anchor, window open, day not yet closed, no reflection; close awards the mock HABIT XP once, and the reflection save patches the `ritualDay` cache directly). Driving spec: [`2026-07-24-daily-closing-ritual-design.md`](../superpowers/specs/2026-07-24-daily-closing-ritual-design.md); ADR [0010](../decisions/0010-gamified-growth-xp-feedback-not-payment.md).

## 2. User-facing behavior

### Entry — the Today evening island's Napzárás CTA (`features/today/components/IslandEvening.tsx`, the 🌙 **Este** island's hero act slot — since the three-islands re-composition, `mezo-euze`, [ADR 0022](../decisions/0022-today-three-islands.md); `RitualCard` is deleted)

Three states derived from `useRitualDay` + the pure `ritualWindowState(now, window)` (`features/ritual/logic/ritualWindow.ts`) — the derivation is unchanged from the `RitualCard` era, only its rendering dissolved into the island's hero:

- **waiting** (before `window.opensAt`) — a quiet ghost button `Napzárás {opensAt}-kor nyílik` (`.td-ghost`, `DaypartEvening.tsx:122-126`) in the island's act row; the hero sub reads `napzárás {opensAt}-től · villanyoltás {bedTime}`. The ghost still navigates to `/ritual` — the window *nudges*, it never locks.
- **open** (from `opensAt` onward — a **soft, ~12h-wide** window, never re-closes itself before the next day) — the **lavender `Zárjuk le a napot` CTA** (`.td-cta.is-lav`, `DaypartEvening.tsx:117-121` — the app's one non-coral CTA) → `navigate('/ritual')`.
- **done** (the day is closed) — the CTA yields to a `Napzárás kész ✓` done line (`.td-foot`, `DaypartEvening.tsx:133`).

**The soft gate stays a nudge, not a lock** (ADR 0010, D2 of the spec): a direct `/ritual` visit works any time today, and since `mezo-euze` the waiting state's quiet ghost is again an in-app route into `/ritual` — the opening time on its label carries the "not yet" signal, so it is not a dead control (the `ItemRow` doctrine the `mezo-j7u4` no-CTA cut protected). **Still recorded:** the hero CTA is Today's single Napzárás surface — `IslandEvening` deliberately filters the `ritual:day` normalizer row out of its L1 (`OWNED_BY_RITUAL_HERO` + the `source !== 'ritual'` guard) because a row pill would be the weaker, non-window-aware duplicate. See [today.md §9](today.md).

The `?ritual=waiting|open|done` URL param (`useTodayScenario().ritual`) **wins over the derived state** — the `?day=` demo-affordance precedent, survives real mode by design for QA/demos.

### The evening habit row ([habit.md §2](habit.md))

The EVENING chain's 4th row, **`evening_ritual`** „Napzárás", is a DERIVED habit whose `Logolás` **navigates to `/ritual`** (`logic/habitAction.ts` maps `evening_ritual` to `{ kind: 'nav', to: '/ritual' }` — the same `nav` kind `morning_weigh_in`/`morning_coffee`/`morning_workout` already use, not a new `ritual`-specific kind). The habit completes derived once the ritual closes (§5). **The mapping is unchanged, but the row no longer renders on Today** (`mezo-j7u4`; filter relocated by `mezo-euze`): `IslandEvening.tsx`'s `OWNED_BY_RITUAL_HERO` filters it out of the evening L1, since the island's Napzárás hero CTA already owns the same act with a stronger affordance. It is still rendered by Growth's „Rutin" tab, so the second entry point moved rather than disappearing — on Today the hero is now the single one.

### The 6 acts (`RitualPage.tsx`, state machine over `act: 1..6`, progress dots `● ● ○ ○ ○ ○`, ✕ exits to `/today` anytime)

1. **Megérkezés** (`ArrivalStep.tsx`) — night-sky takeover, a breathing **clay moon** (`i-hold`), the fixed lines „A nap véget ért." / „Zárjuk le együtt." (byte-exact per the approved mockup — do not paraphrase), `Kezdjük` CTA into act 2. No data. **Since the Mozaik night pass (`mezo-d20.8.1.1`, §11)** the CTA's trailing 🌙 is gone — the moon it stood in for is on the stage as clay.
2. **A napod íve** (`DayStoryStep.tsx`) — the `DayArc` Bézier reprised large (`buildArcPoints`/`pointXY` reused from `features/today/logic/dayArc.ts`, `workoutTime: null` — the workout gets its own recap row instead of a second dot), the arc's check-in dots pop in staggered, then the day's recap event rows from `useDayRecap` (§3). On a thin day (< 2 done recap events **and** < 2 check-ins) a soft acceptance line renders — „Ma ennyi fért bele. Az is számít." — **never** a gap/failure list (ADR 0010).
3. **Ma milyen volt** (`ReflectionStep.tsx`, W1.2 / `mezo-b3pp.2`) — one textarea under „Milyen volt a napod valójában?", voice input through the shared `useVoiceInput` push-to-talk chip (the `JournalSheet`/`ChatPage` composer idiom — transcription appends to whatever is already typed), seeded as **initial state only** from the day's existing `reflectionText`, so re-entering the act shows what was written while a background refetch can never overwrite live typing. „Tovább" fire-and-forget-`PUT`s the prose and advances immediately (a failed save — e.g. a stale tab's 409 — is swallowed rather than trapping the user mid-ritual); „Ma nem írok" skips in one tap and writes **nothing at all**, whatever is in the box. This is the **one write before the Harvest act** — an idempotent upsert on `(created_by, ritual_date)` that cannot conflict with the close, which only stamps `closed_at`. The save fires **on advance only, never debounced per keystroke** (§9), and only when the trimmed prose differs from the seed — which is also what makes *clearing* an existing reflection reachable from the UI (an emptied box PUTs `''`, the CLEAR payload). The W1.3 gratitude rows sit in this same act below the textarea (**W1.3b**, `mezo-b3pp.25` — spec §5.2's combined writing act: one act, both halves optional). They follow the prose's two rules exactly — written on advance only, never per keystroke, and fire-and-forget so a failed `POST /api/journal/gratitude` cannot trap the user — and „Ma nem írok" writes **neither** half. Their one extra rule is the day cap: the act reads today's entries back (`useGratitudeEntries(date, date)`), renders them under the „Amiért hálás vagy" eyebrow as a plain `<ul>` (`aria-label="Ma már elmentett hálabejegyzések"` carries the "already saved" meaning for screen readers, since nothing about the markup otherwise distinguishes it from the editable rows below), and offers only `3 − saved.length` input slots (at 3, none at all). A GET that fails outright is treated like a still-pending one — no input rows are opened, since `useDualQuery`'s `realEmpty: []` would otherwise read a failed fetch as "zero saved today" and hand back all three slots — and a short „A mai hálabejegyzéseid most nem érhetők el." line says so. The ritual is forward-only, but a ✕-and-re-enter replays act 3 from act 1, and without the read-back a second pass would duplicate the evening's lines. There is no gratitude `PUT`, so a saved line is shown, never offered for edit.
4. **Nyitott hurkok** (`LoopsStep.tsx`, the only loop-closing act) — closes the day's two **gated** open loops via `openLoops()` (§3): a missing check-in slot (`Koppints` → signals `onOpenCheckIn`, the page mounts `CheckInSheet`) and, only when the day has ≥ 1 intention focus, the evening reflection (inline `Igen`/`Részben`/`Nem` buttons calling `useIntentionActions(date).reflect` directly — no sheet). When neither is open: „Minden hurok zárva ✓". A **third, evergreen** row — „Történt még valami ma?" → `Napló` — always renders, is never glowing, and never participates in the "nothing open" check (you can always log one more thing). `Tovább` always advances regardless of loop state — nothing here is mandatory.
5. **Termés** (`HarvestStep.tsx`, the peak) — on **entering** this act, `RitualPage` fires `useRitualActions(date).close(ringsOf(states))` exactly once (a `closedRef` guard) — **since `mezo-dhzk`** the call also carries the day's six Életjel-ring values (`useNeeds` snapshotted once at page mount, `ringsOf` from `features/today/logic/needsInputs.ts`), so the same close that ends the day also stores the needs day-close snapshot and awards its bonus XP (see [needs.md §5](needs.md#5-integrations)); the habit read then completes `evening_ritual` (+10 XP). The stage shows: a `CountUp` XP total, per-source chips (`GET /api/gamification/day/{date}`'s `xpBySource`, HU-labelled QUEST/HABIT/ACTIVITY/GYM/RUN/SPORT — 5 other wire members MEAL/WEIGHT/SLEEP/CHECKIN/MEDICATION are defensively skipped, not rendered unlabelled), coin chips (`coinEvents`), an optional LIFE skill-bar highlight (the skill with the highest within-level progress that hasn't maxed out — see §9 for why the "N XP to next level" hint is dropped), the streak flame (clay `i-lang`; dim + "— megszakadt" when `!streakAlive`, the `AppHero` dead-streak-copy precedent), and — **new, `mezo-dhzk`** — a second clay-`i-eletjel` "N napja életben" line from `useNeedsSummary()` whenever the needs streak is > 0 (the two streaks are independent: this one tracks all-six-rings-green closes, not the habit/ritual streak above it). Confetti (10 particles, transform-only) bursts around the streak beat only when `xpTotal > 0` — a thin day gets no confetti. The reward block is **vertically centered** (the `rz-harvest-body` wrapper, `margin-block: auto`) with the `Tovább` CTA pinned to the bottom (`mezo-tr5v`) — no top-loaded empty space; `Tovább` advances to act 6.
6. **Elengedés** (`ReleaseStep.tsx`) — the arc closes into a full circle around the **clay moon** (`i-hold`, the same symbol act 1 opened on), the fixed closing line „A nap le van zárva. Elengedheted.", the companion's „napzárás" closing note when present (honest absence otherwise), and a **handoff panel** — „MOST JÖN · ALVÁS-ELŐKÉSZÍTÉS" listing the two remaining stops (lecsendesítés at `prepStartsAt`, villanyoltás at `bedTime` — straight from the ritual window, no new data) + `Esti rutin indítása →`, which navigates to `/today` (the 🌙 **Este** island owns the sleep-prep phase from there — its `windDown`-phase hero content, incl. the winddown `Leállás megvolt ✓` ghost, plus the evening habit chain's L1 `ItemRow`s (`IslandList`, since `mezo-euze`) — integration, not duplication; this component never renders prep-step UI itself).

**The ✕ exit is consequence-free except for a reflection the user explicitly saved in act 3.** No close fires before act 5 — entering Termés is still the single `close()` call site, so exiting at any point before it leaves `closed_at` null and the evening simply lapses (ADR 0010). The one thing that *can* outlive an aborted ritual is the act-3 prose, and only because the user tapped „Tovább" on an edited textarea: that PUT already landed, and it deliberately persists (it is the day's honest record, not ritual bookkeeping). Skipping („Ma nem írok"), advancing an untouched box, or ✕-ing out of act 3 all write nothing. Until W1.2 this invariant read „nothing is written before act 4" — it was relaxed by exactly one endpoint, on purpose (§9).

## 2b. The Mozaik night language (`mezo-d20.8.1.1`, F7.1)

The design round that brought the ritual up to Design 2.0 — spec:
[`2026-08-31-napzaras-mozaik-night-design.md`](../superpowers/specs/2026-08-31-napzaras-mozaik-night-design.md),
prototype: [`napzaras.html`](../design_2.0/prototypes/napzaras.html). The ritual **keeps its
takeover character** — it did not become a light tile mosaic — and takes the Mozaik's
*materiality* instead: clay symbols, tile anatomy, tokenised radii/shadows. The dramaturgy is
light:

- **The darkening arc.** `RitualPage` stamps `data-act` on `.rz-screen`, and
  `--rz-sky-1..6` (declared in BOTH `:root` blocks, guarded by
  `features/ritual/ritualCssTokens.test.ts`) deepen the sky act by act. Act 1 is the Nap tab
  evening panel's own lavender at dusk, which is what dissolves the entry break: the ritual
  dims the room the user was already standing in. Act 6 is the deepest night.
- **The starfield** (`.rz-stars`) holds a fixed 16 stars whose positions are index-derived in
  `RitualPage` — never `Math.random`, because `/ritual` carries visual goldens. Only opacity
  rides the arc, so the sky gains stars by *clearing*.
- **The gold break** (`.rz-glow`, act 5) is how the peak-end beat is told: a warm radial rising
  through the otherwise-dark sky. It renders on every harvest; the confetti above it stays
  earned (`xpTotal > 0`).
- **The night-washed tile** (`.rz-nw` + `.rz-nw-spot`) is the one new primitive — the Mozaik
  tile anatomy on a dark sky. Act 2's recap rows, act 4's loops, act 5's source chips and skill
  row, and act 6's handoff panel are all built from it. An open loop breathes in **lavender**
  (`.rz-loop.glow`), never coral — a loop left open is not a failure (ADR 0010).
- **Clay replaced every ritual emoji** (moon, recap glyphs, loop glyphs, coin/flame/needs
  streak, handoff). `RecapEvent.icon` is now a `ClayIconName`, not a `string` — the glyph choice
  was always presentation the hook happened to carry, and the compiler now catches a symbol
  that isn't in the sprite. Two new symbols joined the asset contract: **`i-hold`** and
  **`i-termes`**.
- **Deliberately untouched:** `levelUpMeta`'s `CHIP_ICON_BY_SOURCE` and `LIFE_SKILLS` emoji.
  Both also feed the LevelUpScreen / Growth / JournalSheet surfaces, which have had no design
  round — one surface moves at a time. The consequence is that act 3's life-area chips are the
  ritual's last visible emoji; the F7.4 Én round owns that decision.

Everything behavioural is unchanged: the six acts, their order, every HU string except the two
that lost a trailing 🌙, the advance-only writes in act 3, the single `close()` on entering act
5, and the consequence-free ✕.

## 3. Architecture & data flow

```
IslandEvening (Este island hero)      RitualPage (act state machine, /ritual)
  → useRitualDay(date)                  → useRitualDay(date)              — window + closed state + reflectionText
      + ritualWindowState(now, window)  → useDayRecap(date)               — act 2 (pure composition, ZERO new fetches)
                                         → useRitualActions(date).saveReflection(text)
                                                                            — act 3, ON ADVANCE ONLY, iff the prose changed
                                         → useCheckins() / useIntentionDay(date)+useIntentionActions
                                                                            — act 4 (reused Today hooks/sheets)
                                         → useNeeds(tickNow)               — snapshotted once at mount (mezo-dhzk)
                                         → useRitualActions(date).close(ringsOf(states))
                                                                            — fires ONCE on entering act 5
                                         → useGamificationDay(date) + useProgressionProfile()
                                             + useNeedsSummary()           — act 5 (the Harvest read)
                                         → useCompanionNote()              — act 6 closing note
      mock: ritualMock.ts (mockRitualDay/EMPTY_RITUAL_DAY)
      real: ritualApi.ts → GET /api/ritual/day/{date} | POST /api/ritual/close
                         | PUT /api/ritual/reflection
              → RitualController → RitualService (getDay/close/saveReflection)
              → SleepAnchorPort.resolve(userId)   — the window's bed anchor
              → RitualDayRepository               — the ritual_day row (closed ⇔ closed_at is not null)
              → RitualClosedEvent ──AFTER_COMMIT──→ ReflectionEmbeddingListener (companion)
                                                      → MemoryEmbeddingWriter.writeReflection
                                                      → memory_embedding(kind=reflection)
```

- **`useRitualDay`** (`data/ritual/ritualHooks.ts`) is a **`useDualQuery`**: mock returns `mockRitualDay(date)` (a fixed 22:30 bed anchor, window open, `closed: false`) synchronously; real returns the honest-empty `EMPTY_RITUAL_DAY(date)` (ghost window mirroring the backend's no-sleep-goal config default, bed 22:00) while unresolved — never the seed. Query key `['ritualDay', date]`.
- **`useRitualActions(date).close(rings?)`** — **since `mezo-dhzk`** `close` takes an optional `NeedsRingsWire` (the day's Életjel-ring snapshot, `ringsOf(useNeeds(...).states)`; `RitualPage` always passes it, so in practice the param is only optional for other/older callers and tests). Mock: patches the cache to `closed: true` idempotently (a second call on an already-closed day is a no-op — "no second award"), ticks the DERIVED `evening_ritual` chain row via `completeMockDerivedHabit(qc, date, 'evening_ritual')` (`@/data/habit/habitHooks`, `mezo-o5hx` — the mock mirror of the server-side `ritual_closed` derivation), calls `awardGamificationEvent(qc, { type: 'HABIT', xpOverride: 10 })` (the intention first-focus precedent), and — only inside the not-yet-closed branch, after the ritual's own award — calls `applyMockNeedsClose(qc, date, rings)` when `rings` is present (`data/needs/needsHooks.ts`; it carries its own `lastCloseDate` idempotency guard too, belt-and-braces against a re-close double-award). Real: POSTs `/api/ritual/close`, then — **best-effort, non-blocking** — `needsApi.close(date, rings)` when `rings` is present (a `try/catch` swallow: a needs-service hiccup must never block the napzárás itself), then invalidates **six** query keys in an **ordered, awaited** sequence, not a concurrent fan-out (`mezo-ywz1`): first `await invalidateQueries(['habitDay', date])` alone — this is the derived-completion refetch (see below), and `RitualPage` mounting `useHabitDay(date)` is what gives it an active observer to actually refetch (not just mark-stale); only once that settles does `['gamificationDay', date]` + `['gamification']` + `['progressionProfile']` invalidate together (`Promise.all`, since the +10 they need to read now exists); `['dailyQuests', date]`, `['ritualDay', date]`, and `['needsSummary']` invalidate last, fire-and-forget (not harvest-critical). The mock path is unaffected by this ordering — it's a synchronous cache patch, no invalidation race is possible. Full needs-award rule (per-ring + all-green bonus + streak) documented in [needs.md §4](needs.md#4-data-model--api); this doc covers only how the ritual close **carries** the snapshot.
- **`useRitualActions(date).saveReflection(text)`** (`mezo-b3pp.2`) — the act-3 prose upsert, the mutation that is **not** a close. Mock: patches `['ritualDay', date]` to `{ ...prev, reflectionText: text.trim() || null }` with no server round trip. Real: `PUT /api/ritual/reflection` and **reseeds the cache from the response** (`setQueryData`, not an invalidation — the response already IS the day, so a refetch would be a wasted round trip mid-flow) and invalidates nothing else: no habit, gamification or needs key can move on a reflection. Blank/whitespace text is a **clear**, mirroring the backend's `strip()`-then-null-if-blank exactly so the two modes cannot diverge on what an emptied textarea means. `pending` is the OR of both mutations.
- **`useDayRecap(date)`** (`data/ritual/recapHooks.ts`) is the act-2 "day story" — a **pure composition over already-fetched domain hooks** (`useCheckins`, `useActivities`, `useIntentionDay`, `useFuelDay`, `useTrain`, `useSleep`, `useWeight`, `useCompanionNote`), **zero new fetches**, mirroring the `useToday` composition precedent (sanctioned cross-domain hook composition in the data layer, not a new backend read). It derives `{ events, checkinsDone, thinDay, closingNote }`: training (real done-state from `completedTodayWorkout`, mock **always** `done: false` — no fabricated completion signal) or, on a rest day, a `today`-flagged volleyball session; a single meals-aggregate event (protein sum); weight **only if today has a logged entry** (no stale-row fallback); sleep from the date-less always-latest `useSleep`; one event per activity-log entry; one event per intention focus (its `done` mirrors the day's single holistic reflection, not per-focus state). Supplements are **deliberately skipped** — `useFuelDay().fuel.supplements` is the static demo list in **both** modes (never swapped for a real read), so deriving an event from it would fabricate data in real mode (§9). `thinDay` = fewer than 2 done recap events **and** fewer than 2 check-ins; `closingNote` surfaces only a companion note of `kind === 'closing'`.
- **`openLoops({ checkins, intention })`** (`features/ritual/logic/openLoops.ts`, pure) derives `{ checkinOpen, reflectOpen }` — `checkinOpen` reuses the TodayPage next-open-slot predicate; `reflectOpen` is `foci.length > 0 && reflection == null` (no focus at all ⇒ never open, nothing to reflect on). The journal invite is a deliberate third row **outside** this shape — evergreen, never "closes".
- **`ritualWindowState(now, window)`** (`features/ritual/logic/ritualWindow.ts`, pure) — **wrap-safe**: everything is computed as minutes-forward-from-`opensAt` (never lexical HH:mm comparison), so a bed time past midnight (e.g. `opensAt: 23:15`) still derives correctly. `'open'` = within the 12h window forward of `opensAt`; a companion `minutesUntil(now, hhmm)` helper is shared by both.
- **`harvestStages({ sources, coins, hasSkillHighlight })`** (`features/ritual/logic/harvestStages.ts`, pure) — the act-5 reward-choreography **schedule only** (no timers/animation itself): fixed cadence `xp-total` at 400ms → each source chip +250ms → the coin run starting 300ms after the last pre-coin beat, +250ms per coin → an optional skill highlight +400ms after → the streak flame always closing the sequence +400ms after whatever came before. `HarvestStep` staggers its `CountUp`/pop-in `animationDelay`s directly off this schedule.
- **Habit read-triggered close-evaluation:** `HabitEvaluator`'s `ritual_closed` metric (`INTRADAY_METRICS`) re-checks `RitualDayRepository` (closed-only finder) on every today-read, so the EVENING chain's `evening_ritual` row flips `pending → done` on the very next `useHabitDay` read after close — no polling, the read is the trigger (the `useDailyQuests`/`useHabitDay` `staleTime: 0` precedent, see [habit.md §3](habit.md)). `RitualPage` mounts `useHabitDay(date)` itself (an otherwise-unused-return mount, `staleTime: 0` in real mode) purely to give `['habitDay', date]` an active observer **during the flow**, so that read fires as part of `close()`'s invalidation instead of waiting for the next `/today` visit (`mezo-ywz1`).
- **Level-up suppression:** entering act 5 fires `close()` then `useHabitActions(date).consumeLevelUps()` — the Harvest stage IS the celebration for the ritual's own habit completion, so any habit levelUp sitting in the cache is silently dropped rather than also firing the global `LevelUpScreen` overlay a second time once the user lands back on `/today`. **Covered by the FE test suite (MSW-mocked real mode) in both modes, and verified live on the real Postgres stack (R4, `mezo-ywz1`, 2026-07-25): after `close()` the Harvest advanced 0 → 10 XP with the ritual's own `☀️ Rutin +10`, and returning to `/today` flashed no second `LevelUpScreen` (that run's +10 did not cross a level threshold, so the suppression branch itself remains exercised by the FE suite's level-up scenario).** In REAL mode this depends on the `mezo-ywz1` fix above: `RitualPage`'s `useHabitDay(date)` mount gives `close()`'s `['habitDay', date]` invalidation an observer to refetch, and `close()` **awaits** that refetch before resolving, so by the time `.then(() => consumeLevelUps())` runs, the ritual's own level-up is already sitting in the cache to be dropped — not a stale cache from before the close.

## 4. Data model & API

**Table `ritual_day`** (migration [`202607241000_mezo-hvmx_create_ritual_day.sql`](../../backend/src/main/resources/db/changelog/1.0.0/script/202607241000_mezo-hvmx_create_ritual_day.sql), originally an `intention_creed`-style existence-record): `id uuid pk`, `created_by`, soft-delete cols, `ritual_date date`, `closed_at timestamptz`, `reflection_text text`. **Since `mezo-b3pp.2`** ([`202608211500_mezo-b3pp.2_ritual_day_reflection.sql`](../../backend/src/main/resources/db/changelog/1.0.0/script/202608211500_mezo-b3pp.2_ritual_day_reflection.sql)) the row is **no longer an existence record**: the evening reflection upserts it BEFORE the close, so `closed_at` is **nullable** and "the day was closed" is `closed_at is not null` — never mere row existence. Every reader moved to the closed-only finders in the same change (`RitualService`, `HabitEvaluator`, `MetricSeriesService`); `RitualDayRepository` carries `findByCreatedByAndRitualDateAndClosedAtIsNotNull`, `findByCreatedByAndRitualDateBetweenAndClosedAtIsNotNull`, `findFirstByCreatedByAndClosedAtIsNotNullOrderByRitualDateAsc`; the only plain finder left beside them is the singular `findByCreatedByAndRitualDate` (the row-identity lookup the close and the reflection upsert both need). The old semantics-ambiguous range/first-row finders (`findByCreatedByAndRitualDateBetween`, `findFirstByCreatedByOrderByRitualDateAsc`) were **deleted** — a ritual range read must not have a wrong-semantics twin to grab by mistake. Partial unique `uq_ritual_day_user_date (created_by, ritual_date) where is_deleted = false`. In the `ResetDatabase` TRUNCATE list (`support/ResetDatabase.java:40`).

**`RitualService`** (`feature/ritual/service/RitualService.java`, gated `mezo.feature.ritual.enabled`): `getDay(userId, date)` — pure read, **never 404** for a valid date, composes `{date, closed, closedAt?, window}` where `window` (`opensAt`/`prepStartsAt`/`bedTime`) derives fresh from `SleepAnchorPort.resolve(userId).bed()` on every call (`bed − leadMin` / `bed − prepLeadMin` / `bed`, the `HabitTargets` idiom — ghosts the port's config default when no sleep goal exists). `close(userId, date)` — **today only** (else 409 `RITUAL_NOT_TODAY`); reuses the (created_by, ritual_date) row when one already exists — including an **open, reflection-only** row, which it stamps closed rather than inserting a second row against `uq_ritual_day_user_date` (`mezo-b3pp.2`) — and inserts an OPEN row (`insertOpenRow`, `closed_at` left null) otherwise. The `closed_at == null` stamp branch is therefore the **single closing site** for both paths, which is what gives `RitualClosedEvent` exactly one publication point; **idempotent** (an already-closed row has `closed_at != null`, so the stamp — and the event — are skipped and `closedAt` is unchanged). **Awards nothing itself** — completion XP rides the habit's own `HABIT` tail (§5). `saveReflection(userId, date, text)` — **today only too** (the same 409; the prose is written *inside* the evening ritual, and a wider write surface would only let past days sprout rows), upserts `reflection_text` on the same row, `strip()`ping a non-blank body (a textarea's trailing newline must not give otherwise identical prose a different embedding vector) and mapping blank/whitespace-only to `null` — **a clear, never a create**: with no row present a blank save returns the honest empty day and writes nothing at all, so an abandoned textarea leaves no junk row behind. When the row is already closed the save re-publishes `RitualClosedEvent` so the vector cannot go stale (§5).

**No concurrent-insert recovery, deliberately (`mezo-b3pp.2`).** The old `insertOrReread` caught `DataIntegrityViolationException` and re-read the winner's row (the `HabitService.ensureRows` precedent); `insertOpenRow` **does not**, and the catch was deleted rather than carried over. `uq_ritual_day_user_date` can only fire if a concurrent request committed the same `(created_by, ritual_date)` first, and by then Postgres has marked *this* transaction aborted — the re-read inside the catch would itself fail with "current transaction is aborted", i.e. it looked like recovery while never recovering. The loser now 500s and the retry finds the row. mezo is single-user, so this needs two of the owner's own writes in the same millisecond; making it survivable would mean a `REQUIRES_NEW` insert bean, which buys that back at the cost of committing orphan rows whenever the outer transaction rolls back.

**`closed` means `closed_at is not null`, not "a row exists" (`mezo-b3pp.2`) — and this binds every future reader.** Before W1.2 the `ritual_day` row *was* the closure record, so a plain existence check was a correct closure check. The reflection upsert can now create that row hours before the close, so three readers moved together in the same change: `RitualService.toResponse`, `HabitEvaluator`'s `ritual_closed` metric (`findByCreatedByAndRitualDateAndClosedAtIsNotNull`), and `MetricSeriesService.ritualClosed` — **both** halves of it, the adoption lookup (`findFirstByCreatedByAndClosedAtIsNotNullOrderByRitualDateAsc`) and the lit-day set (`findByCreatedByAndRitualDateBetweenAndClosedAtIsNotNull`), since an open row must neither start the series early nor light a day up. **Any future `ritual_day` reader must use a closed-only finder**: `findByCreatedByAndRitualDate` survives only as the row-identity lookup the close and the reflection upsert need, and it now answers "did the evening start", not "did the day close". The two old range/first-row finders were deleted outright so no wrong-semantics twin is left to grab by mistake.

## 5. Integrations

All backend dependencies are **pure reads** — ritual depends on {sleep goal}; nothing backend-side depends back on ritual **except** habit's evaluator read (a one-way pure read, not a cycle).

- **← Sleep goal** (`RitualService`, via `SleepAnchorPort`): the window's `bedTime`/`opensAt`/`prepStartsAt` derive fresh from the shared sleep-anchor port (the `HabitTargets` idiom) — no sleep-goal-side change, ghosts the port's config default (bed 22:00) when no sleep goal exists.
- **↔ Habit** ([habit.md §5](habit.md)): **ritual → habit is one-way** — `HabitEvaluator`'s `ritual_closed` metric reads `RitualDayRepository` directly (a plain JPA bean, the intention-repos precedent), via the **closed-only** finder since `mezo-b3pp.2` — a reflection alone must not complete the habit; the ritual domain never reads habit. The FE side mirrors this: the EVENING chain's `evening_ritual` row keeps its `/ritual` `nav` mapping (`habitAction.ts`), and closing the ritual invalidates `['habitDay', date]` so it flips to `done` on the next read — visible on Growth's Rutin tab and in the Este face's chain progress, though the row itself is filtered off Today (§2). The habit's sleep-prep siblings (`wind_down`, `bed_on_time`) are **not** part of the ritual — they come after it (D3 of the spec).
- **→ Companion (narrative memory), `mezo-b3pp.2`** ([companion.md](companion.md), the reflection embedding seam): the close's stamp branch publishes **`RitualClosedEvent`** — a plain `publishEvent` **inside** the writing transaction, so the commit boundary is the consumer's job — which the companion-owned **`ReflectionEmbeddingListener`** (`@Async` + `@TransactionalEventListener(AFTER_COMMIT)`, `@ConditionalOnProperty` array-AND'ed on **both** `COMPANION_SWITCH` and `RITUAL_SWITCH`, so either switch off means the bean does not exist and no embed call can happen) consumes: it re-reads the row **by id** (never carrying the prose on the event, so a close plus a fast follow-up edit both embed the LATEST text) and calls `MemoryEmbeddingWriter.writeReflection` → `memory_embedding(kind=reflection, ref_id=ritual_day.id, occurred_on=ritual_date)`. **Embedded on close, not per keystroke** — the reflection is the fifth populated `memory_embedding` kind and the only one whose write trigger is a *different* domain's lifecycle event. A post-close edit re-publishes the same event and re-embeds the `(kind, ref_id)` row **in place** (the `journal_entry`/`decision` update-in-place mechanics, [journal.md §9](journal.md)); clearing the prose **soft-deletes** the vector, so an erased evening stops being recallable (IDENT-3 honesty) — and because the unique index is not partial, that dead row keeps its `(kind, ref_id)` slot, so writing prose **again** afterwards must REVIVE it (`upsert` reads through a native `findByKindAndRefIdIncludingDeleted` and clears `is_deleted`) rather than insert a colliding one; reflection is the first kind that can reach clear-then-rewrite at all ([journal.md §9](journal.md)). Failures are logged and swallowed — memory building must never break the close. The ritual side owns no companion import: it publishes a `feature/ritual` event and the listener lives in `feature/companion`, the same direction the journal/decision seams already run.
- **← Intention, Check-in, Activity** (act 4, `LoopsStep`): reuses `useIntentionActions(date).reflect` ([intention.md](intention.md)), `useCheckins().saveCheckIn` + the shared `CheckInSheet` ([today.md §2](today.md)), and `ActivityLogSheet` ([growth.md §2](growth.md)) — **one write path per signal**, mounted at the `RitualPage` level exactly like `TodayPage` mounts `CheckInSheet`. No new write surface is introduced; the ritual only signals which sheet to open.
- **← Proactive** (act 6, `ReleaseStep`): renders the existing 20:30 heartbeat "napzárás" companion note via `useCompanionNote()` when present, honest absence otherwise ([proactive.md](proactive.md)) — no proactive-side change.
- **→ Progression:** the close's own XP rides the existing **HABIT** award tail (`evening_ritual`'s catalog XP) — **no new progression source type**. The Harvest stage reads `level_up_event`-derived aggregates (via the gamification day endpoint) + `useProgressionProfile()` for the skill-bar highlight; it introduces no new progression contract.
- **→ Gamification** ([growth.md §2](growth.md)): the ritual's Harvest stage (`HarvestStep`) is the **primary UI consumer** of `GET /api/gamification/day/{date}` via **`useGamificationDay(date)`** — the hook existed since R1 with no UI consumer; R3 is what wires it up. It reads the day's own `xpBySource`/`coinEvents`/`streakDays`/`streakAlive` snapshot deliberately (not `useGamification()`'s account-level read) — the two mocks intentionally diverge (day seed vs. account-profile seed) since the Harvest act reports *tonight's* numbers, not a stale account figure. **In REAL mode**, the Harvest total/`☀️ Rutin` chip now includes the ritual's own +10 `evening_ritual` closure XP (`mezo-ywz1`): `close()` awaits the `['habitDay', date]` refetch (which `RitualPage`'s `useHabitDay(date)` mount makes actually happen) before invalidating `['gamificationDay', date]`, so the derived award is persisted before the Harvest re-read fires. Covered by the FE test suite (MSW-mocked real mode) in both modes, and verified live on the real Postgres stack (R4, `mezo-ywz1`, 2026-07-25): closing the ritual on a clean `demodata` account moved the Harvest total 0 → 10 and rendered the `☀️ Rutin +10` chip (the streak also advanced 6 → 7), confirming the awaited `habitDay` → `gamificationDay` sequence lands the derived +10 before the re-read.
- **→ Today:** since `mezo-j7u4` Today has a **single Napzárás entry point** — since `mezo-euze` that is the 🌙 Este island's hero CTA (`IslandEvening`, §2; formerly `RitualCard`); the `evening_ritual` row is filtered off Today in its favour, and the `ritual:day` normalizer row with it. `useDayRecap` only **reads** existing Today-adjacent hooks, adds nothing to `useToday`. **Today's own day model now shares this domain's clock:** `features/today/logic/dayFace.ts` derives its three daypart windows from the same `useSleepGoal()` wake/bed anchor `ritualWindow.ts` uses, so the Este island and the Napzárás window can never disagree about when evening is. Today also still hosts the arc: `DayStoryStep` imports `buildArcPoints`/`pointXY` from `features/today/logic/dayArc.ts`, whose own component was deleted — see [today.md §9](today.md).
- **↔ Theme (design system, `mezo-tr5v`):** the `rz-screen` is a hard-dark takeover regardless of theme, but the sheets it portals (`CheckInSheet`, `ActivityLogSheet`) and any XP-award overlay (`LevelUpScreen`) are theme-aware and would render **light** for a light-mode user — clashing. `RitualPage` therefore calls `useTheme().setForceTheme('dark')` on mount and `setForceTheme(null)` on unmount (a transient override that does **not** touch the persisted `mode`), so the whole flow — including every portaled surface — renders dark, then reverts to the user's real theme on exit. See [`_platform-design-system.md` §5](_platform-design-system.md) (theme cascade).
- **↔ Account progression / `AppHero` (mock-mode side-effect):** the mock close call is one more call site of the mock account-XP precedent ([growth.md §2](growth.md)) — real mode never calls it (account XP is derived from the profile).

## 6. How to use it (consume)

```ts
import { useRitualDay, useRitualActions, useDayRecap } from '@/data/hooks'

const { data: ritualDay } = useRitualDay(date)          // RitualDay — { closed, reflectionText, window }
const { close, saveReflection, pending } = useRitualActions(date)
await close()                                            // idempotent; real mode invalidates 6 keys
await close(ringsOf(needs.states))                       // + best-effort needs day-close snapshot (mezo-dhzk)

await saveReflection('Nehéz nap volt, de kibírtam.')     // upsert; reseeds ['ritualDay', date]
await saveReflection('')                                 // CLEAR — never a create (mezo-b3pp.2)

const { events, checkinsDone, thinDay, closingNote } = useDayRecap(date)  // pure, zero new fetches
```

```ts
import { ritualWindowState } from '@/features/ritual/logic/ritualWindow'
const state = ritualWindowState(new Date(), ritualDay.window)  // 'waiting' | 'open'
```

- Never import `ritualApi`/`ritualMock` directly — go through `@/data/hooks`.
- `useRitualDay` never 404s and has no ghost-guard obligation — the window is always present (config-ghosted absent a sleep goal). `IslandEvening` still branches on `ritualDay.closed` for its `Napzárás kész ✓` done state.
- `saveReflection` is **today-only server-side** (409 `RITUAL_NOT_TODAY`, mirroring `close`) and is called by exactly one component, `ReflectionStep`, on advance. Call it with `''` to clear; do not "helpfully" skip an empty call, because empty is how the user erases yesterday's-evening prose they took back.
- Query keys: `['ritualDay', date]`; a real close write invalidates `['ritualDay', date]` + `['habitDay', date]` + `['dailyQuests', date]` + `['gamificationDay', date]` + `['gamification']` + `['progressionProfile']` + (since `mezo-dhzk`) `['needsSummary']`.
- `useDayRecap` composes several other domains' hooks — do not call it more than once per screen if avoidable (each call re-runs the same composition; TanStack Query dedupes the underlying fetches by key, so this is a CPU/readability concern, not a network one).

## 7. How to extend it

- **Add a 7th act:** insert a new `act` branch in `RitualPage.tsx`'s render switch, bump `ACT_COUNT` (5 → 6 in `mezo-b3pp.2`; the progress dots and `RitualPage.test.tsx`'s dot-count assertion both read it), renumber the acts *after* the insertion point in the page, the `.rz-*` CSS comments and the visual click-through, and place the new component under `features/ritual/components/`. ## 9. Decisions, gotchas & deferred

- **W1.2 decisions (`mezo-b3pp.2`, spec §5.2) — the evening prose reflection:**
  - **Today-only writes, mirroring the close.** `saveReflection` reuses `close`'s date guard and its 409 `RITUAL_NOT_TODAY` rather than accepting any date: the prose is written *inside* the evening ritual, so a wider write surface would buy nothing but the ability to make past days sprout rows. The FE never sends a foreign date; the guard is there for a stale tab.
  - **Blank is a CLEAR, never a create.** Whitespace-only text nulls the column when a row exists and writes *nothing* when it doesn't — an abandoned textarea must not leave a junk `ritual_day` row that then has to be reasoned about by every closed-only finder. Non-blank text is `strip()`ped before storage so a trailing newline can't give otherwise identical prose a different embedding vector.
  - **Save on advance, never a debounced autosave.** `ReflectionStep` writes only when the trimmed prose differs from the value seeded at mount. Two reasons, and the second is load-bearing: (1) an autosave firing alongside the close could put two events into the embed-insert branch concurrently, and `ReflectionEmbeddingListener` deliberately omits the insert-race retry its sibling listeners carry precisely because "embedded on close, never on every keystroke-save"; (2) comparing against the *seed* rather than against emptiness is what makes clearing reachable at all — a user who wrote prose, backed out, re-entered and deleted it must have that erasure persist, or the close would embed prose they took back. „Ma nem írok" is **not** a clear: skipping means "don't touch today's entry".
  - **Embed on close, not on save.** The vector is written when the day closes (and re-written when a closed day's prose is edited), not on every PUT — see §5. That is also why `RitualClosedEvent` has exactly two publication sites and why the repeat-close path publishes nothing.
  - **Fire-and-forget save.** The act advances without awaiting the PUT and swallows its rejection: nothing in the writing act may trap the user inside the ritual (IDENT-3).
  - **The gratitude half shipped as W1.3b (`mezo-b3pp.25`).** Spec §5.2 describes one combined writing act; this slice originally shipped only the prose half, with the insertion point marked in `ReflectionStep`'s doc comment (§7) — the gratitude rows landed later, below the textarea, reusing `GratitudeRows` ([`journal.md`](journal.md) §2).
- **Decision (W1.3b, `mezo-b3pp.25`) — the gratitude rows are capped by reading today's saved entries back, not by local act-only state.** A ✕ exit is available on every act, and re-entering the ritual always replays from act 1 (§6), so a user who wrote gratitude lines, exited before the Harvest close, and came back in would hit act 3 again with its rows reset to empty local state — nothing about the write would tell the second pass what the first pass already saved. Note what this is *not* guarding against: `JournalSheet`'s own „Hála" mode is freely reopenable (the QuickInput „Hála" tile, `/me/naplo`'s new-entry path) and does no read-back of its own, so more than 3 lines a day is trivially reachable through the sheet — the sheet and the backend (`GratitudeService.create`) both accept it. The read-back exists here specifically because act 3 is replayed *involuntarily*: leaving the ritual with ✕ and re-entering always restarts at act 1 and walks back through act 3, so without a read-back the same evening's already-written lines would be silently re-offered and re-written. The "1–3 a day" cap is a ritual-act rule, not a product-wide invariant. `ReflectionStep` closes that gap by reading `useGratitudeEntries(date, date)` on every mount: already-saved lines render under the „Amiért hálás vagy" eyebrow as a plain `<ul>` (no gratitude `PUT` exists to edit them, so showing is the only option; `aria-label="Ma már elmentett hálabejegyzések"` gives the list an accessible name so "already saved" isn't purely a visual distinction from the rows below), and only `3 − saved.length` input slots are offered — zero at the cap, so a third re-entry after two prior saves shows no row at all. A failed read is never treated as an empty day (`useDualQuery`'s `realEmpty: []` would otherwise make a 500 look identical to "nothing saved yet" and open all three slots) — it renders zero slots and an honest „A mai hálabejegyzéseid most nem érhetők el." line instead. The same `slice(0, slots)` re-check happens again at advance time, in case a concurrent write landed an entry while the act was open.
- **Decisions (spec §3 D1–D10, as of R3's 5-act flow — act 3 and a sixth act joined in W1.2):** full-screen flow, `AppLayout` child with hidden tab bar (D1); sleep-anchored soft gateway — the window nudges, never locks (D2); the ritual is the evening home, **composing, not duplicating** existing surfaces (D3); small closure XP through the existing HABIT tail, no new progression source (D4); two backend slices — R1 gamification ledger + R2 thin ritual domain (D5); no duplicate XP table — `level_up_event` IS the ledger (D6); read-time streak + idempotent coin awards (D7); CSS-first animation, no new dependency (D8); two entry points — the Today ritual entry + the habit row (D9; **the Today half was narrowed by [ADR 0014](../decisions/0014-today-daypart-faces.md) and re-homed by [ADR 0022](../decisions/0022-today-three-islands.md)** — the evening island's hero CTA is Today's only entry point, the habit row survives on Growth's Rutin tab, §2); warm-night + lavender visual direction (D10). Tone: ADR [0010](../decisions/0010-gamified-growth-xp-feedback-not-payment.md).
- **Gotcha — supplements are skipped from the day recap.** `useFuelDay().fuel.supplements` is the static demo list from `data/fuel/fuel.ts` in **both** modes (never swapped for a real read anywhere in `fuelHooks.ts`) — rendering a "supplements n/m" recap beat from it would show fabricated data in real mode, so the honest-absence rule wins over the design spec's aspirational beat (§4 of the spec lists it; it was deliberately dropped in implementation).
- **Gotcha — the skill "N XP to next level" hint is dropped.** The spec's Harvest copy imagines "még N XP a Lv M-ig"; the wire `SkillLevel` type only carries `level` + `progressPct` (the within-level fill), no per-skill XP-to-next-level curve (`gamification/levelCurve.ts`'s `xpToNext` is the **account**-level curve, not per-skill, and no other FE surface derives this figure either). Fabricating N from `progressPct` alone would require an assumed XP-per-level the FE cannot honestly know, so `HarvestStep` renders only the bar + `Lv N`.
- **Gotcha — the title-unlock beat is deferred.** The spec's Harvest-act table mentions "title unlock if due"; no title-unlock detection or celebration exists in `HarvestStep` — out of scope for R3, not wired to any hook yet.
- **Gotcha — the Harvest streak reads the DAY snapshot, not the account snapshot.** `HarvestStep` deliberately uses `useGamificationDay(date)`'s own `streakDays`/`streakAlive`, not `useGamification()`'s account-level read — the two mock seeds intentionally diverge (day: 12/alive vs. account: 6/alive) so a future consumer doesn't accidentally conflate them.
- **Gotcha — `habitAction.ts` has no dedicated `ritual` kind.** The spec's §7 language ("a new `ritual` kind") is realized as the existing generic `nav` kind with `to: '/ritual'` — no new `HabitAction` variant was needed since `nav` already generalizes "go to this route."
- **Gotcha — level-ups are suppressed on ritual close, covered by the FE test suite in both modes and verified live (R4, `mezo-ywz1`).** Entering the Harvest act (4 then, 5 since `mezo-b3pp.2`) calls `consumeLevelUps()` right after `close()` so Today's habit level-up effect (`TodayPage.tsx:136-141`, formerly `RoutineCard`'s) never fires the global `LevelUpScreen` overlay a second time back on `/today` — the Harvest stage IS that celebration. Real mode depends on `RitualPage` mounting `useHabitDay(date)` (giving `['habitDay', date]` an active observer) and `close()` awaiting that key's invalidation before resolving (`mezo-ywz1`) — without the observer, the invalidation is a no-op refetch-wise and `consumeLevelUps()` runs too early. This ordering is exercised by the MSW-mocked FE test suite in both modes, and was verified live on the real Postgres stack (2026-07-25): closing the ritual advanced the Harvest to the +10 `☀️ Rutin` and returning to `/today` showed no second `LevelUpScreen` (`RitualCard` flipped to its done state, the EVENING chain to 1/6 · +10 XP). That run predates the daypart faces; the suppression mechanism is unchanged (the effect moved from `RoutineCard` to `TodayPage`, same consume-once call), but note the observed surfaces are now the Este island's Napzárás done line + the L1 chain rows (`mezo-euze`). This run's +10 did not cross a level threshold, so the suppression branch itself stays covered by the FE suite's level-up scenario. A future act that also grants XP (if ever added) must respect this suppression, not fight it.
- **Journal invite is evergreen by design** (`openLoops.ts`'s doc comment) — gating it on "already logged today" would make it permanently vanish in mock mode (the mock `useActivities` seed is date-invariant) and contradicts its "always askable" nature, unlike the two scheduled loops.
- **Gotcha — real-mode close→habit→harvest ordering, fixed (`mezo-ywz1`).** Originally no component on `/ritual` observed `['habitDay', date]`, so `close()`'s invalidation of that key was a no-op refetch-wise in real mode: the ritual's own +10 `evening_ritual` XP and its level-up only landed in the cache on the next `GET /api/habit/day` (i.e. after returning to `/today`), not during the flow. Fixed by (1) `RitualPage` mounting `useHabitDay(date)` purely for its observer side effect, and (2) `useRitualActions(date).close()`'s real path awaiting `['habitDay', date]`'s invalidation **before** invalidating `['gamificationDay', date]`/`['gamification']`/`['progressionProfile']`, instead of firing all six invalidations concurrently. Any future ritual-adjacent derived-completion-on-read pattern must follow the same observer-mount + ordered-await shape, not assume `invalidateQueries` alone guarantees a refetch.
- **R4 (`mezo-mzbz`) — done:** the `/ritual` visual goldens (Arrival + Harvest, frozen clock, both themes, darwin+linux — `tests/visual/visual.spec.ts`) and the formal reduced-motion audit landed. **Audit result:** every active animation on a ritual-family selector is neutralized under `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` — 10 via an explicit `animation: none` in the `rz-*` tail reduce block (`.rz-moon`/`.rz-cta`/`.rz-ring`/`.rz-circle-moon`/`.rz-handoff-cta`/`.rz-arc-path`/`.rz-arc-dot`/`.rz-pop`/`.rz-skill-bar i`/`.np-anim`), and the `.rz-conf` confetti children via the container's `display: none`. (The 11th at audit time was `.ritcard-moon`'s breathing, guarded by a `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference` opt-in — **that rule no longer exists**: the `.ritcard*` family was deleted in `mezo-j7u4`. The guard test still scans for `.ritcard` selectors, so it stays correct-by-vacuity for that family and would catch one if it ever came back; Today's own replacement motion family — since `mezo-euze` the island families (`isl-morph`/`isl-floaty`/`isl-rowin`/`isl-phasein`, the retired `.faceswap`'s successors) — has its own guard, `features/today/todayReducedMotion.test.ts`, [today.md §8](today.md).) — enforced going forward by `features/ritual/reducedMotionGuard.test.ts` (§8), which fails on any newly-introduced unguarded ritual animation. The `CountUp` reduced-motion/jsdom immediate-value path is covered by `CountUp.test.tsx`. **Out of scope (spec §11):** PWA push/notification reminder at window opening; hard-gating sleep-prep behind the ritual; a "day grade" self-rating (the intention reflection + 20:00 check-in already carry the evaluative signal); weekly/monthly recap acts (Insights Weekly + Memoir own that horizon); companion-generated dynamic copy beyond the existing heartbeat note; retroactively closing a past day (a missed ritual lapses quietly, ADR 0010).
