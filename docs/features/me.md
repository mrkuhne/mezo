# Me Area — Feature Documentation

> The profile + personal-biometrics + relationships hub. **Mixed status:** `Súly` (weight) and `Alvás` (sleep) are ✅ Phase-2 backed (the `biometrics` backend feature); `Cél` (the long-term goal) is ✅ active-goal backed; `Emberek` (People) is ✅ backend-backed since Slice E (`mezo-t16y.2` — `person`/`mention` tables, dual-mode `usePeople`); the **Én hub** renders backend biometrics + progression + a live line per tile (the identity hero reads the signed-in account since S6 — §9); `Tudás` (Knowledge) facts are ✅ backend-backed since companion V1.2 (Insights-domain dual-mode `useKnowledge`; the graph/edges layer stays mock). Lives under the **`Én` tab** — one of the five first-class tabs of the Design 2.0 IA ([ADR 0032](../decisions/0032-five-tab-ia-dissolved-section-shells.md)). **`/me` is no longer a section shell with a sub-nav: it is the `EnHubPage` Mozaik hub** (`pages/EnHubPage.tsx`, `mezo-d20.6.1`) — an identity hero + goal card + **seven small tiles plus a full-width Rutin tile** (`mezo-o486` hub-tile-reorg, 2026-09-01, plus the `mezo-3zue.3` Rutin tile), whose tiles navigate to **full-page siblings** on their stable `/me/*` routes. The `MeSection` shell, the shared `AppHero`, the `SubNavDropdown` and the `ME_TABS` array (`pages/tabs.ts`) are all **deleted** (`mezo-d20.9.1`), and `Profil` as a *page* is gone — the hub absorbed it. The old Hungarian sub-nav order no longer matches the hub: the **hub-tile-reorg** (`mezo-o486`, 2026-09-01, spec [`2026-09-01-hub-tile-reorg-design.md`](../superpowers/specs/2026-09-01-hub-tile-reorg-design.md)) pulled `Heti`, `Tudás`, `Karakter`, `Értesítés` and `AI-napló` off the Én hub — Súly/Alvás/Growth/Napló/Emberek stayed, and a new **Beállítások** tile (→ `BeallitasokPage`, `/me/beallitasok`) replaced the old settings band; see §2. `Heti` (weekly-review slice `mezo-p2tr`) RETIRED the old Insights „Heti" tab ([insights.md §2.2](insights.md)) and, since the reorg, lives **only** on the Mezo hub (it briefly also sat on the Én hub — that duplicate is gone). **`Growth` (`/me/growth`, `mezo-rmhr`) is the dedicated home of the whole XP universe** — all-band skills + a 30-day quest/activity journal + computed badges/perks — reached from the hub's Growth tile (§2; the growth **domain** — quests, activity log, XP economy — lives in [growth.md](growth.md)).
>
> **Placeholder strip (`mezo-lfw`):** the Phase-3 "vision" UI was removed so every screen reflects only what actually works. The four backend-backed views (`Profil`/`Cél`/`Súly`/`Alvás`) were cut to **backend-data only** — all narrative/decorative surfaces gone (Mezo "mit látunk" insight cards, "Hatások" factor cards, every decorative `ToolChipRow`, the Profil identity hero / identity-goal quote / PERMA bars / quick-setting rows / version footer, the `SettingsSheet` notification overview, the goal hero "Vége" projection stat, the `TrendCell` delta/ON-TRACK legs, the sleep weekly cells + chart target overlay + hero "Target" comparison). The two no-backend views (`Emberek`/`Tudás`) were **trimmed** to a working mock shell (kept: People log/grid/feed, Knowledge fact list; removed: credit hero, ritual/attention/relation-pattern cards, IDENT-5 footer, the "Gráf nézet hamarosan" stub, tool chips). Net: ~1.2k lines deleted, 13 view-local components dropped, the `WeightTrends`/`SleepTrends`/`PeopleSummary` mock shapes pruned. See §2/§4/§9.
>
> **Mozaik 2.0 (Design 2.0, `mezo-d20`, 2026-08-29) — the third render layer over an unchanged model.** After the placeholder strip (`mezo-lfw`) and the Napív re-skin (`mezo-8141`/`mezo-mifi`), this area has now been re-faced a third time: the iOS-list vocabulary gives way to a **tile mosaic** ([ADR 0033](../decisions/0033-mozaik-2-tile-language.md), which supersedes ADR 0026) and the section shell dissolves into a hub + full-page siblings ([ADR 0032](../decisions/0032-five-tab-ia-dissolved-section-shells.md)). **What changed is the view layer only.** Every hook in §3, every endpoint in §4, every seam in §5 and every honesty contract (`—` for a null statistic, an absent card rather than an empty one, the `<2`-subscore gate, the `isComplete` goal gate) is carried through **verbatim** — the F5 slices were explicitly required to re-render the existing contracts, not to re-decide them. Read §2 for the new faces and §3–§5 for the model they render, which is the same model this doc has described since Slice A.

## 1. Summary

The **Me** area is the user's personal hub: who they are (the **`Én` hub** itself), their long-term body-weight goal & strategy (`Cél`), their daily weight log + trend (`Súly`), their sleep log (`Alvás`), the people they track (`Emberek`), and a knowledge facts surface (`Tudás`). It exists as the "self & relationships" counterpart to the activity-focused tabs (Nap, Edzés, Fuel, Mezo).

## 2. User-facing behavior

**The Me section shell is gone (`mezo-d20.6.1`, cleaned up in `mezo-d20.9.1`).** `MeSection.tsx`, `pages/tabs.ts` (`ME_TABS`), the shared `AppHero` (`features/progression/components/AppHero.tsx`) and `shared/ui/SubNavDropdown.tsx` are all **deleted** — there is no `<Outlet>` under `/me` any more, and `router.tsx` registers every `/me/*` view as a flat sibling route. Navigation between them is **the hub's tiles going down and each page's `‹ Én` back chip coming up**, plus the five-tab `TabBar` (`app/TabBar.tsx`: Nap · Edzés · Fuel · Mezo · Én) and the floating coral quick-log FAB (`app/QuickLogFab.tsx`) that ride above every route. The `MeOutletContext` (`{ openSettings }`) was already retired in S7; the settings sheet is now owned by `EnHubPage` itself. Rationale and the full IA: [ADR 0032](../decisions/0032-five-tab-ia-dissolved-section-shells.md); the tile/clay/token vocabulary: [ADR 0033](../decisions/0033-mozaik-2-tile-language.md) and [`_platform-design-system.md`](_platform-design-system.md).

### `Én` hub (`pages/EnHubPage.tsx`) — ✅ the `/me` index (`mezo-d20.6.1`)
**This page replaced `ProfilePage`, which is deleted.** Source of truth: `docs/design_2.0/prototypes/src/en-body.html` hub section (prototype px ×1.18). Top to bottom:

- **Header** — no longer this page's own: since `mezo-atry` it is the app shell's single `AppHeader` (`frontend/src/app/AppHeader.tsx`, mounted by `AppLayout`, [today.md](today.md#the-header-is-the-shells-not-the-hubs)), identical on every hub — section label + clay spot · daypart switch (always navigates to `/nap`) · Mezo-messages circle → `/nap/uzenetek` · notification bell (unread badge, three-row „Értesítések · ma" popover, „Összes értesítés ›" foot into `/me/ertesitesek`) · profile orb → `/me`. `EnHubPage` itself renders none of this.
- **Identity hero** — an `.enh-idring` XP ring around the name's initial (`--xp` = `xpInLevel / xpForNext`, `role="img"` with a spoken „Szint N — X% a következő szintig"), the name, the equipped title chip, and a `Lv · XP · 🔥 nap · 🪙` stat row off `useProfile()`/`useGamification()`/`useTitles()` — a **dimmed streak** (`opacity 0.45`) when `streakAlive === false`. This is where the retired `AppHero`'s identity role went; the ring/level/title/counters are the same account-progression reads ([growth.md](growth.md)), just no longer platform chrome mounted on five sections.
- **Bio line** — `MeBioRow`'s contract **inlined verbatim**: `·`-joined non-null bits (age from `birthDate` · height · latest logged weight · body-fat%), the whole line vanishing at zero bits. Tapping it opens `BiometricSheet`. **At zero bits the hero substitutes `BiometricCard`'s own empty-state CTA** („Állítsd be a biometriád") rather than dropping the button — otherwise retiring the Biometria card would have silently retired the only biometrics *write* path with it. The derived split-TDEE block the card used to render (`Alaphő · NEAT` / `Betábl. mozgás` / `Fenntartó`, opening the Fuel-owned `EnergyBreakdownSheet`) has **no home on the hub** — a real regression, recorded in §9.
- **Life-goal hero (`mezo-iizd.4`)** — the hero is the **life goals'** summary, not the weight goal's. Eyebrow `🎯 Célok` + an `{n} aktív` chip; below it up to four `lg-goalchip`s (one per active goal, washed by its PERMAH dimension); below that an `MCells` trio **emelkedik** (sage, `↗ {n}`) / **tartja** (lavender, `→ {n}`) / **csúszik** (coral, `↘ {n}`), counted off `useLifeGoalToday()`'s per-goal arrows — with `insufficient` deliberately excluded from all three buckets (too little data must never masquerade as a direction). **The mosaic's per-day sub-jelek (`mezo-jcpt.5`).** `WeekDayTile` renders **six** bars per day, not the historical four — `DAY_DIMENSIONS` (`features/me/logic/weekDay.ts`, key/label/barClass/`group: 'do'|'be'`), the SAME list `WeekDayPage`'s dimension tiles use (§2 "Day page" below), so the weekly mosaic and the day page share one vocabulary and one palette instead of two. The six bars split into **two groups of three** — `do` (nutrition/quality/training — "what you did that day") and `be` (sleep/logging/rhythm — "how your body/rhythm stands") — with a wider gap between the groups (a `.wkd-sparks i.is-gsep` marker on the bar after the last `do` entry) and a matching `.wkd-legsep` divider in `WeekDaysPage`'s legend, so the grouping reads without needing the legend at all. Each bar is `is-<key>` (a domain hue reused from the day page's palette — sage/gold/coral/lavender/rose/sky) or `is-none` for a null dimension. **`rhythm` is deliberately excluded from every "how much did we measure" test** (`INTRINSIC_SUBSCORE_KEYS`, five of the six keys) — it is *extrinsic*, the mean of OTHER days' base scores (`DayEvaluationEngine` §3), so it can carry a value even on a day nothing was logged; `subscoreCount`/`isEmptyDay`/`dayState` all route through `INTRINSIC_SUBSCORE_KEYS` so that `rhythm` alone can never flip an untouched day from **„nincs adat"** to **„tanulom"**. Against the real backend, an untouched day now genuinely renders **„nincs adat"**, not „tanulom" (`mezo-el0t`): `DayEvaluationEngine.anyLogPresent(DayInputs)` is the one predicate answering "did the user log anything at all that day" (meals/kcal, water, check-ins, sleep duration, sleep quality, completed workouts, weight, XP), and `loggingDim` only degrades to `NO_DATA` (`null`, not an honest `0`) when that predicate is false — so `logging` IS `null` in production for a fully untouched closed day, and `subscoreCount(day) === 0` (`weekDay.ts:64`) holds against the real backend exactly as it does against the mock. A day that logged something elsewhere (trained, slept, weighed in) but skipped meals/water/check-ins still gets `logging`'s honest `0` and reads „tanulom", unchanged from before. The mock's all-null Szombat day (`meWeek.ts:85`) is simply one instance of this shape, not a mock-only special case anymore. **This backend/frontend agreement holds only for a truly untouched day.** A day whose only fact is a planned-but-skipped workout is the one exception: the day page's `DayEvaluationEngine` reads it `empty` (see [companion.md §3](companion.md)), but the mosaic still derives `thin`, because `training`'s config weight is still on the `me-week` wire and `subscoreCount` counts it regardless of the backend's `NO_DATA` status for that dimension. Known, tracked disagreement — `mezo-jcpt.16` — not fixed here.

**Chat handoff (Task 10, `features/me/logic/useChatHandoff.ts`).** `WeekAnalysisPage`'s „Beszélgess a hétről" and `WeekDayPage`'s „Beszélgess a napról" chips both call `useChatHandoff().open({kind: 'day'|'week', date})`: real mode POSTs `chatApi.createConversation({context: {kind, date}})` (`POST /api/companion/conversation` — [companion.md §4](companion.md)) and navigates to `/mezo/chat?c={id}`, where the **server** has already generated an opening turn anchored on that week/day; mock mode fabricates the same shape locally (a canned Hungarian opening line + a seeded conversation) with no network call. **Re-entrancy guard:** `open()` is a no-op while a real create is in flight (`if (pending) return`, `useChatHandoff.ts:76`) — a structural guard inside the hook itself, not merely a disabled-button convention every call site must remember; every call site also threads the returned `pending` flag into its own `disabled`+spinner for the visual layer.

## 3. Architecture & data flow

> **Design 2.0 changed nothing in this section.** Not one hook, query key, mapper, invalidation fan-out or ownership rule below was touched by the Mozaik re-face — the F5 slices re-rendered these reads, they did not re-plumb them. The only structural difference is *who calls them*: `EnHubPage` now calls **sixteen `@/data/hooks` reads at once** (`useToday`, `useProfile`, `useGamification`, `useTitles`, `useBiometricProfile`, `useWeight`, `useGoal`, `useMeWeek`, `useSleep`, `useProgressionProfile`, `useGratitudeEntries`, `useDecisions`, `usePeople`, `useKnowledge`, `useNotificationPrefs`, `useLlmUsageSummary`) plus `useNotificationFeed`, where the old `ProfilePage` called four and each sub-tab called its own. They all share TanStack Query's cache, so the hub costs the same requests the pages themselves would have made — but a new tile line means a new hook call on the hub, and that is the one place a careless addition can make the Én tab's cold load heavier than any single page's.

The single FE↔data boundary is **`frontend/src/data/hooks.ts`**. Each hook branches on `isMockMode()` (`frontend/src/data/_client/mode.ts` — `import.meta.env.VITE_USE_MOCK !== 'false'`, **default mock**). Views import only from `@/data/hooks`, never deeper. (The barrel is app-wide shared — other domains' re-export additions, e.g. the `mezo-cd8s` train review hooks, the `mezo-ilsj` ritual/recap hooks or, most recently, **`useFeedback`** (Phase 5 W4.1 `mezo-b3pp.15` — the 👍/👎 hook behind the companion-owned `/api/companion/feedback`, consumed by Insights + Today, [`_platform-data-layer.md` §2](_platform-data-layer.md)), move this key_file **without changing anything Me consumes**. **Since `mezo-p2tr` one Me surface DOES carry feedback chips** — `Heti`'s `WeekReviewCard`/`WeekNextCard` vote on the generated weekly review/suggestion (§2/§5.12) — every other Me tab still renders no AI-generated artifact; their AI-adjacent pages are the *audit* reads (AI-napló, Knowledge graph), which show what the model did, not model output to rate.)

**Sleep goal + day-anchor (`useSleepGoal`/`useSleepGoalActions`, `data/me/sleepHooks.ts:40-71`, `mezo-dbsr`):** the day's wake/bed anchor is now a first-class **sleep goal**, not a field buried in the weight goal. `useSleepGoal()` is a `useDualQuery` (key `['sleepGoal']`) whose `realEmpty` is the **config-ghost** `SLEEP_GOAL_GHOST` (WAKE 06:00 / 480 min → bed 22:00, `isSet: false`, `data/me/sleepGoal.ts`) — the backend `GET /api/sleep/goal` **never 404s**, so the ghost is the honest pre-save value, never the mock seed. `SleepGoal.isSet` (`mezo-k0hp`) travels with it: `composeSleepGoal(input, isSet = true)` defaults to a chosen goal, and only the ghost passes `false`, so an unresolved read and an unset goal are the same honest shape. `SleepGoalInput` omits it (`wakeTime`/`bedTime`/`isSet` are all server-owned). `useSleepGoalActions().setGoal` PUTs `/api/sleep/goal` and invalidates **`['sleepGoal']` + `['habitDay']` + `['fuelDay']`** (`sleepHooks.ts:61-65`) — the wake/bed habits re-centre and the Fuel meal slots cascade off the new anchor. Mock mode composes the goal in-cache via `composeSleepGoal`. The FE derivation `deriveSleepTimes` (`data/me/sleepGoal.ts:8`) mirrors the backend `SleepAnchorResolver.derive` exactly (WAKE→bed = wake − target; BED→wake = bed + target, mod 24h) — the single wake/bed formula, held in one place per layer (D1/D4).

**Profile:** `useProfile()` (`data/me/meHooks.ts`) — **since S6 (`mezo-qw37.6`)** real mode reads `GET /api/auth/me` via `useMe()` and returns `null` until it resolves (ghost-guard, no seed fallback); mock mode still returns `{ user }` from the static `data/today/today.ts` seed. The `identityGoal`/`areas`/`quickSettings`/`notifSettings`/`appVersion` exports and `data/me.ts` were **deleted** with the Profil strip (`mezo-lfw`). The Slice-E "static is fine" decision is now closed — see §9.

**People (`usePeople`, `data/me/peopleHooks.ts` + `peopleApi.ts`, Slice E `mezo-t16y.2`; candidate split S4 `mezo-06o0.3`):** a **dual-mode bootstrap** (`useDualQuery`, key `['people']`) returning `{ people, candidates, mentions, logMention, decidePerson, isPending }` — signature-stable with the old mock hook plus the S4 additions. `people` filters out `status === 'candidate'` rows; `candidates` is the complement — the two are always disjoint views over the same bootstrap payload, never a second fetch. `decidePerson(personId, decision: 'accept' | 'reject')` is a `useMutation`: real POSTs `{ decision } satisfies PersonDecisionRequest` to `/api/people/{personId}/decision` and invalidates `['people']`; mock edits the cached person in place (`accept` → `status: 'active'`, `reject` → the person is filtered out of the cache, mirroring the server's soft delete). Mock: the `data/me/people.ts` seed synchronously. Real: `GET /api/people` → `{ persons, mentions }` mapped by `toPersonEntry`/`toMention` (`peopleApi.ts`) onto the mock-era domain shapes; **display labels are FE-derived** (`mentionDayLabel`: Ma/Tegnap/weekday/hu month-day; `lastMentionLabel` = "day · time", or `'Még nincs említés'` when the person has no mentions). `logMention(input: MentionLogInput)` is a `useMutation`: real POSTs `{tone, text}` to `/api/people/{personId}/mentions` and invalidates `['people']` (server-truth refetch); mock prepends a client-built `Mention` into the cache (the medicationHooks pattern).

**Backend ownership path:** `WeightLogEntity`/`SleepLogEntity`/`GoalEntity`/`BiometricProfileEntity` all extend `OwnedEntity` (`techcore/persistence/OwnedEntity.java`); the owner is resolved server-side in the controller (`currentUserId.get()` — injected `CurrentUserId` bean, JWT subject → UUID) and stamped onto the entity (`e.setCreatedBy(...)`), never from the client. `OwnedRepository.findAllOwned(createdBy)` filters `created_by` + `is_deleted=false`, ordered `date ASC` (used by weight/sleep). **`goal` and `biometric_profile` are date-less owned aggregates**, so their repositories extend `JpaRepository` directly (not `OwnedRepository`, which requires an `e.date` field) and declare bespoke `findBy…CreatedByAndDeletedFalse` finders (`GoalRepository.java:14-19`, `BiometricProfileRepository`).

**Sleep screenshot → draft** (`mezo-66ab`, `api/feature/sleep-shot/sleep-shot.yml`; tag `SleepShot` → `SleepShotApi`; `SleepShotController implements SleepShotApi`, gated on `mezo.feature.sleep-shot.enabled`):
- `POST /api/sleep/screenshot` — **multipart** `photo` (jpeg/png/webp, ≤5 MB — `mezo.sleep-shot` → `SleepShotProperties`) → `SleepShotDraftResponse` (all extraction fields **nullable** — honest partial; only `confidence`+`needsReview` required). `SleepShotService.extract` (`SleepShotService.java:47-68`) runs **ONE** multimodal vision call over the screenshot, brace-window-parses the JSON (the meal/pantry pipeline idiom), zero-pads clock times (Sleep Cycle renders `0:42`), and sets `durationH = asleepMin/60`. **Deterministic `SleepShotDraftValidator`** (`SleepShotDraftValidator.java`, the LLM never grades itself) scores consistency — asleep ≤ in-bed, phase-sum within ±10% of in-bed, span ≈ in-bed ±15 min, HH:mm parse — as `confidence = passed/applicable`; `needsReview` when `confidence ≤ threshold` (boundary-inclusive, config `0.6`) **or** a key field (bedtime/wakeup/asleep) is missing.
- **Hypnogram extraction (`mezo-fk9a`).** The prompt's tenth field asks for one letter per 15 minutes of the screenshot's stage graph (`D`eep/`L`ight/`R`EM/`A`wake), decided by the curve's **colour** at each slot, not its height — colour is categorical, which turns a measurement problem into a recognition problem. `SleepShotDraftValidator.acceptedHypnogram` then gates it independently of `score` (three checks, all-or-nothing — any failure returns `null`): **V1 alphabet** (`[DLRA]+`), **V2 length** vs. the midnight-wrapped bedtime→wakeup clock span (±2 buckets), **V3 composition** — for **all four** stages, `abs(count×15 − actualMin) <= max(30, 0.35×actualMin)` against the exact minute totals; the precondition is `deepMin`/`lightMin`/`remMin`/`awakeMin` **all** present (an uncheckable hypnogram is not worth drawing). The accepted sequence rides back as the shared **`Hypnogram {bucketMin, stages}`** schema (`api/common/common-schemas.yml`), referenced from `SleepShotDraftResponse`, `LogSleepRequest`, and `SleepLogResponse` alike — one schema, three fragments. Full rationale: [ADR 0015](../decisions/0015-hypnogram-display-only.md).

**Weekly review data layer** (`mezo-p2tr`, `api/feature/me-week/me-week.yml`; tag `MeWeek` → `MeWeekApi`; `MeWeekController implements MeWeekApi`, gated on the companion switch):
- `GET /api/me/week/{start}` → `MeWeekResponse {start, days: MeWeekDay[7], weekly: MeWeekAggregates}` — always exactly 7 day entries `start..start+6`; **400 `ME_WEEK_START_NOT_MONDAY`** when `start` is not an ISO Monday (`MeWeekController.java`). Live for the current (in-progress) week — every field is code-computed on read, nothing is pre-materialized.
- `MeWeekDay` carries the day's **live values** (kcal/protein/carbs/fat consumed + kcal/protein targets — `null` when nothing was logged that day, not zero; weight; sleep minutes + quality; check-in count + energy avg; workout count = gym+sport+run sessions; XP) **plus** `score`/`subscores` from `DayScoreService` (below). **`subscores` (`MeWeekSubscores`) is the day-engine's six dimension-ids** (`mezo-jcpt.5` — `nutrition`/`quality`/`training`/`sleep`/`logging`/`rhythm`, each `0–100` or `null` when degraded/no-data), the SAME vocabulary the day-evaluation endpoint uses — not the old four-signal shape. `MeWeekAggregates` is the weekly rollup and is **unchanged by `mezo-jcpt.5`**: `score` (rounded mean of the week's non-null day scores, `null` below the 2-score gate), `prevWeekScore` (the same rollup one ISO week earlier), fuel/sleep/check-in averages, `checkinRatio` (filled slots ÷ `4 × elapsedDays` — `elapsedDays` is 7 once the week has fully passed, `today − start + 1` clamped to `[1,7]` while it's in progress, so a live in-progress week's ratio isn't diluted by days that haven't happened yet, and `0` — turned into a `null` `checkinRatio`, never a fabricated `0.0` — for a fully-future week where `today` is still before `start`), `latestWeightKg`, `weightWeeklyRateKg` (the same EWMA `WeightTrendService.computeTrend` rate `Súly` reads), `totalXp`.
- `GET /api/me/week/{start}/trend?weeks=8` → `MeWeekTrendResponse {start, weeks, points: MeWeekTrendPoint[]}` (`mezo-d20.7.5`) — the Heti hero's score trend. The window **ends at `start`** (inclusive) and counts back `weeks` ISO weeks (`1..26`, default 8, validated in the contract), so the trend follows the *browsed* week rather than always ending at today; same **400 `ME_WEEK_START_NOT_MONDAY`**. Each `MeWeekTrendPoint` is `{weekStart, score, sleepAvg, fuelAvg, checkinAvg, activityAvg, computedAt}`, **oldest first**. A week without a score yields **no point** — the series is shorter than the window, never padded with a zero.
- **The persisted weekly score** (`weekly_score`, `feature/companion/{entity/WeeklyScoreEntity,repository/WeeklyScoreRepository,service/WeeklyScoreService}.java`) — its **own table, not columns on `weekly_review`**: the score is a deterministic function of the week's logs while the review narrative is not, so an empty week or a failed LLM call must still be able to carry a score. `period_summary` was ruled out (narrative store, no numeric field).
  - **The write rule — write-through, refresh-on-read.** Every full computation of a week upserts the row (`WeeklyScoreService.record`, called from `MeWeekService.week`), which keeps the cache warm from all three paths that already compute a week: the `/api/me/week/{start}` read, `WeeklyReviewGenerator.gather` and `WeekContextRenderer` on every chat turn. There is **no scheduled writer**.
  - **The row is a cache, not a truth** — a retroactive log changes a past week's score. Every cached read (`scoreFor`, `trend`) first probes whether that week saw a score-relevant write after `computed_at` (`WeeklyScoreRepository.latestScoreInputWrittenAt`, one native MAX over `sleep_log`/`check_in`/`meal`/`meal_item`/`water_log`/`workout_session`/`sport_session`/`run_session_log`/`habit_day`/`activity_log`/`daily_quest` — `water_log` joined the probe in `mezo-jcpt.4` once `logging` started reading it) and recomputes when it did; a week that has **not finished yet** is always recomputed. `computedAt` travels into the contract, because a cache that cannot say when it was computed is not honest. Known limitation (inherited from `WeeklyReviewService.isStale`): the probe reads `created_at`, so an **edit** of an existing row is not detected. `weight_log` is deliberately excluded — weight feeds no dimension. **Accepted limitation (`mezo-jcpt.4`):** the training **schedule** tables (`gym_schedule_slot`/`sport_schedule_slot`/`sport_event`/`running_block` and the prescribed runs under it) are deliberately OUT of the probe too — they are configuration, edited in place with no dated row in the probed window, and one edit can retroactively re-plan every past week; a schedule edit alone therefore does NOT invalidate a cached week, which keeps the training dimension's plan/done split from re-triggering a recompute on every schedule tweak. **One-off cache purge:** migration `202609031200_mezo-jcpt.4_weekly_score_cache_invalidation.sql` deletes every `weekly_score` row once, because existing rows held pre-engine numbers (most visibly `checkin_avg`, whose successor dimension `logging` now scores an untouched day as an honest 0 where the old subscore was `null`) that the freshness probe alone would never catch (it only re-triggers on a moved LOG, not on a formula change).
  - **No logs in the window ⇒ nothing is computed.** The probe's null answer is an exact proof of "no score": sleep needs a `sleep_log`, fuel a `meal`, check-in a `check_in` and activity a workout/XP row, so with none of them present no day can clear the 2-subscore gate.
  - **Honest absence.** A null weekly score writes **no row** (and deletes an existing one), so an absent score is absent — never a stored 0.
  - **What it replaced:** `prevWeekScore` used to cost a **second complete score run** (nine metric-series queries + seven fuel roll-ups) on *every* week read; it now reads this cache.
- **`DayScoreService`** (`feature/companion/service/DayScoreService.java`) — `MeWeekService`'s single source for both `MeWeekDay.score`/`.subscores` and the weekly `score`/`prevWeekScore` rollups. **The formula itself moved** (`mezo-jcpt.4`, plan 2/2): there is now exactly ONE day math in the codebase, `DayEvaluationEngine`'s pure 6-dimension evaluation (nutrition/quality/training/sleep/logging/rhythm — full formula, honesty rules and the day-evaluation endpoint: [companion.md §3/§4](companion.md)). `DayScoreService` still projects the evaluation's six dimensions onto a `DaySubscores` carrier — but **since `mezo-jcpt.5` that projection is a straight 1:1** (`nutrition`/`quality`/`training`/`sleep`/`logging`/`rhythm`, the same six dimension-ids, onto the wire's `MeWeekSubscores`), not the old four-key remap. The **only** place a four-key shape survives is `WeeklyScoreEntity`'s four persisted cache columns (`sleep_avg`/`fuel_avg`/`checkin_avg`/`activity_avg`) — those stayed put deliberately (D3, no migration): `WeeklyScoreService` still narrows the six-dimension `DaySubscores` down to four for that table, via the SAME closest-successor mapping the wire used to use: `sleepAvg ← sleep`, `fuelAvg ← nutrition`, `checkinAvg ← logging`, `activityAvg ← training`; `quality` and `rhythm` deliberately get no cache column. The overall day score is the engine's `base` — still `null` under the same `<2`-present honesty gate, now counted over 6 dimensions instead of 4. **Sleep quality and check-in energy still normalize as `(v-1)/9`** (1–10 dials on `SleepLogSheet`/`CheckInSheet`) — that piece of the formula carried over bit-for-bit.

None of it is Me-owned; Me is a consumer. **The decorative `ToolChipRow` + per-view `FACTOR_TOOLS`/`PATTERN_TOOLS`/`KNOWLEDGE_TOOLS` `Tool[]` arrays were removed from every Me view (`mezo-lfw`)** — they named *intended* Phase-3 AI tool calls and rendered decoratively (never executed). `ToolChipRow` itself survives (used by Fuel/Train/Insights), it just no longer appears in Me.

### 5.7 Growth ↔ Me (read-only consumers — the `/me/growth` page + summary card, `mezo-rmhr`)
The Profil **`GrowthSummaryCard`** and the **`/me/growth` page** are the Me-side surfaces of the **Growth** domain (daily quests + activity log + gamified progression, [growth.md](growth.md)). They only **consume** the progression backend — `useProgressionProfile()` (all-band skills + `traits` + `savingsHuf30d`) plus the Growth page's three history/achievements reads (§3) — and share `ATHLETIC_META`/`LIFE_SKILLS`/`MUSCLE_LABELS` for the display labels (`features/progression/logic/levelUpMeta.ts` + `data/train/train.ts`). The XP that fills them is earned on **Today** (the activity log + quest cards), never from Me; Me owns no growth write path. (`radarGeometry` retired with the radar cards — the summary + Skillek tab are meter rows, no SVG geometry.)

### 5.8 Me ↔ Notification platform (wired, N1+N2+N3 all shipped)
`Értesítés` is Me's only surface for the cross-cutting **notification** platform layer ([`_platform-notifications.md`](_platform-notifications.md), `mezo-h4wp.6.1`/`.6.2`/`.6.3`) — it owns no notification data itself, only drives the settings UI. **Contracts crossing the seam:** `PushSubscriptionState` (N1, unchanged — incl. `error: PushErrorCode | null`) from `usePushSubscription()`; `NotificationPrefView[]` (N2) from `useNotificationPrefs()` (`data/notification/notificationPrefHooks.ts`, a normal `useDualQuery` read, unlike N1's device-owned hook); the page also composes `useCheckins`/`useStack`/`useProtocol`/`useSleepGoal`/`useTrain`/`useRunning`/`useRitualDay`/`useMedication` (all already used elsewhere in Me/Fuel/Train) purely to derive the preview header + per-row sub-lines client-side — no new reads exist just for this page. No other Me view reads or writes push state. The dispatcher itself (`NotificationDispatchJob`, reading the proactive tables + Today/Ritual/Train anchors) lives entirely in `feature/notification` — see the platform doc §3/§5/§9.

### 5.9 Sleep/Weight logging → Proactive companion feed (wired, one-way OUT, `mezo-gst9`)
`SleepLogService.log`/`WeightLogService.log` (`feature/biometrics/{sleep,weight}/service/`) each publish a
`SleepLogSavedEvent{userId, date}`/`WeightLogSavedEvent{userId, date}` via `ApplicationEventPublisher`, right
before returning, **inside** the `@Transactional` write method — so a listener bound to `AFTER_COMMIT`
only ever sees a log that actually persisted. `feature/proactive`'s `CompanionMessageEventListener`
(`@Async` off the request thread) is the sole consumer: it generates the day's `sleep`/`weight`
companion-feed reaction message within moments of the log landing, gated on freshness (a backfilled
date never triggers). **This is a fire-and-forget, one-way dependency** — `feature/biometrics` defines
and publishes the event with no import of `feature/proactive` at all (a plain Spring
`ApplicationEvent`, not a direct method call), so the write path's latency and success are completely
unaffected by whether the listener exists, is slow, or fails. Neither `Súly` nor `Alvás` renders
anything from this seam — the reaction message surfaces on **Today**'s MezoChip thread, not here. See
[proactive.md §1/§3](proactive.md) (`CompanionMessageEventListener`) and [today.md](today.md)
(`useCompanionFeed`) for the consuming side.

### 5.10 `Napló` ↔ Companion (embed pipeline, wired, one-way OUT, Phase 5 W1.1 `mezo-b3pp.1` + W1.4 `mezo-b3pp.4`)
Every journal create/update/delete publishes `JournalEntrySavedEvent`/`JournalEntryDeletedEvent`;
a companion-owned `@Async AFTER_COMMIT` listener keeps the entry's `memory_embedding(kind=
journal_entry)` vector row in sync through the companion's single `MemoryEmbeddingWriter` write
path — the same fire-and-forget shape as §5.9's sleep/weight → proactive seam. **W1.4 adds the same
seam for decisions:** `DecisionEntrySavedEvent`, published on both create and review, keeps
`memory_embedding(kind=decision)` in sync via `DecisionEmbeddingListener` +
`MemoryEmbeddingWriter.writeDecision` — a review re-embeds the SAME row with the outcome appended, so
what the companion recalls always reflects the latest known outcome. `feature/journal` has **no
import of `feature/companion`** (a plain `ApplicationEvent`, dependency runs the other way) — with
ONE narrow exception in the other direction, kept one-way by a port: `DecisionService` needs the
companion's rendered context-snapshot text at create time, reached through a journal-owned
`DecisionContextPort` that `feature/companion`'s `DecisionContextAssemblerAdapter` implements (ADR
0029 — a direct import here would have closed a `journal ↔ companion` slice cycle, which
`ArchitectureTest.feature_slices_are_cycle_free` catches and rejects as new debt rather than freezing
it). Full detail — including the update-in-place re-embed decision, the port seam, and the
`memory_embedding` kind-CHECK expansion W1.1 carries — lives in [`journal.md`](journal.md) §3/§5 and
[`companion.md`](companion.md) §4. **W1.4 also adds a `decision_review` push category** (fires on the
decision's own `review_due` day, never after) — see [`_platform-notifications.md`](_platform-notifications.md) §4.

### 5.11 `check_in.note` → Companion (embed pipeline, wired, one-way OUT, Phase 5 W1.5 `mezo-b3pp.5`)
The third seam out of the shared `feature/biometrics` package (§5.1 owns the package, §5.9 its
sleep/weight → proactive events), and the only one with **no listener at all**: a `check_in.note`
of at least `mezo.companion.embedding.note-min-chars` (80) characters becomes a
`memory_embedding(kind=checkin_note)` vector, written by the **nightly `DailySummaryJob` note pass**
(`NoteEmbeddingCatchUp` → `MemoryEmbeddingWriter.syncNote`, lifecycle-aware since `mezo-b3pp.26`:
re-embeds in place on drift and reaps the vector when a live check-in row's note is overwritten
down to blank), not by the check-in write path — so nothing in `feature/biometrics/checkin`
changed, and neither `Súly`/`Alvás` nor any Me view renders anything from this seam. The candidate
query is `CheckInRepository.findNoteCandidates`, read by `CheckInNoteSourceAdapter` — which lives in
`feature/companion/embedding/`, **not** here, because implementing the companion-owned
`NarrativeNoteSource` port from inside `biometrics/checkin/service` would close a new slice cycle
(`ArchitectureTest.feature_slices_are_cycle_free`, the §5.10/ADR 0029 failure mode again), while a
plain `companion → biometrics` read is safe. The pass carries no lower date bound, so its first run
backfills existing check-in history; capture happens on **Today** ([`today.md`](today.md)), never
here. Full seam + its one remaining known, bounded gap (a live note edited down BELOW
`note-min-chars` but still non-blank): [`journal.md`](journal.md) §3/§9.

### 5.12 `Heti` ↔ Companion/Proactive (wired both ways, `mezo-p2tr`)
`Heti` is the one Me surface that both **reads a generated AI artifact** and **writes into the chat surface**. **IN:** `useMeWeek`/`useWeeklyReview` read the companion-owned `GET /api/me/week/{start}` and the proactive-owned `weekly_review` endpoints (§4) — the score engine (`DayScoreService`) lives in `feature/companion`, the narrative (`WeeklyReviewGenerator`) in `feature/proactive`, both documented in full at [companion.md](companion.md)/[proactive.md](proactive.md). **OUT:** the chat handoff (`useChatHandoff`) POSTs `chatApi.createConversation({context})` — a Me-originated write into the companion's `ai_conversation` table that anchors a brand-new conversation, which the companion then answers into FIRST (`ChatService.openingTurn`) before the user types anything. **Contracts crossing the seam:** `MeWeekResponse`/`MeWeekDay`/`MeWeekAggregates` (companion), `WeeklyReviewResponse`/`WeeklyReviewDigestResponse` (proactive), `CreateConversationRequest.context` (companion). No other Me view writes into `ai_conversation`, and no other Me view carries `FeedbackChips` (§5.6's "no AI artifact" note is now `Heti`-specific, not Me-wide).

## 6. How to use it (consume)

Import the hook from `@/data/hooks` (never deeper) and destructure. Always ghost-guard real mode (empty arrays / `lastNight === undefined` on first paint).

```tsx
import { useGoal, useWeight, useSleep, usePeople } from '@/data/hooks'

function Example() {
  const { goal, goalResponse, linkedMesocycles, timeline, goalId } = useGoal()  // active goal + raw contract + plan-link timeline
  const { archive, remove, detachPlan } = useGoalActions()     // goal mutations (G4b; dual-mode)
  const { weightLog, weightTrends, logWeight } = useWeight()    // weight log + real EWMA rates
  const { sleepLog, lastNight, logSleep } = useSleep()          // sleepTrends was removed (mezo-lfw)
  const { people, mentions, logMention } = usePeople()          // summary/patterns were removed (mezo-lfw)

  if (!lastNight) return <p>Még nincs alvásadat.</p>   // canonical real-mode guard

  logWeight({ date: '2026-06-14', weightKg: 82.4, note: null })  // WeightLogInput
  logSleep({ date: '2026-06-14', bedtime: '23:10', wakeup: '06:40',
             durationH: 7.5, quality: 8, awakenings: 1, note: null })  // SleepLogInput
  // ...
}
```

Notes:
- **`useGoals()` no longer exists** — it was split into `useGoal()` (goal + `linkedMesocycles`) and `useWeight()` (`weightLog`/`weightTrends`/`logWeight`). A view that needs both calls both (e.g. `WeightPage` calls `useWeight` for the log/trend and `useGoal` for both `goal` [hero start/target/kind] and `goalResponse` [the chart's plan trajectory], `mezo-l82h`; `GoalsPage` calls `useGoal` for the hero and `useWeight` only for `weightTrends`). `useGoal()` is **read-only**; goal **creation** is a separate hook — **`useGoalCreation()`** (G4a, `{ submit, pending }`, drives `GoalPlannerPage`). Editing an *existing* goal in place is still G4b.
- `useProfile()` returns just `{ user }` (real since S6 — `null` until `/api/auth/me` resolves, the static seed in mock mode only; `EnHubPage` is its only consumer since the F8 cleanup) since the Profil strip (`mezo-lfw`). The **biometric profile is a separate hook** — `useBiometricProfile()` (read, empty(all-null)/old-404→null, `isComplete`) + `useBiometricActions()` (upsert), both from `@/data/hooks` (G6, §3) — consumed by the Én hub's bio line + `BiometricSheet` (and reused by the goal-creation hard gate).
- Mutations are **fire-and-forget** — `logWeight`/`logSleep`/`logMention` call `mutation.mutate(input)` and surface no promise or loading flag to the caller today.
- `WeightPage` tolerates an empty `weightLog` because `WeightTrendChart` shows the soft "Kevés mérés…" hint when `<2` points fall in the window (and renders actual-only with no goal), no `WeeklyWeightCard`s render (`groupByWeek([]) === []`), and the hero falls back to `goal?.currentWeight ?? 0` (goal may be `null` since `mezo-72d`); `SleepPage` must guard `lastNight` explicitly. `GoalsPage` guards `goal === null` up front (empty-state CTA).
- Run modes: mock (no backend) — `VITE_USE_MOCK=true pnpm dev`; real (default) — needs the backend on `:8090` with the `demodata` profile (owner seed makes login work; **weight/sleep start empty — they are NOT seeded**; the 5 People persons ARE seeded, with an honest empty mention feed — the demo feed is opt-in `demofixtures`).

## 7. How to extend it

### Add a field to weight or sleep (contract-first — mandatory order)
1. Edit the contract fragment `api/feature/weight/weight.yml` (request + response schema). Follow **[`docs/references/api_contract_conventions.md`](../references/api_contract_conventions.md)** — never hand-write boundary DTOs.
2. Regenerate: `cd api/generate && npm run generate:api` (merge → `api/openapi.yml`); `cd frontend && pnpm generate:api` (→ `src/data/_client/api.gen.ts`); backend Java DTOs regenerate in `./mvnw generate-sources`.
3. Backend: add the column via a **new** Liquibase changeset `{YYYYMMDDHHMM}_{bd-id}_{desc}.sql` (never edit released ones — **[`docs/references/liquibase_conventions.md`](../references/liquibase_conventions.md)**); add the field to `WeightLogEntity`; map it in `WeightLogService.log` + `WeightLogMapper` per **[`docs/references/spring_patterns.md`](../references/spring_patterns.md)**.
4. FE: extend the request build in `biometricsApi.ts`, the input type in `types.ts`, and the sheet UI.
5. Tests: extend `WeightLogServiceIT` (integration-first — **[`docs/references/testing_standards.md`](../references/testing_standards.md)** + **[`docs/references/integration_test_framework.md`](../references/integration_test_framework.md)**) and the FE `goalsHooks.test.tsx` MSW handler. **Both FE modes must stay green.**

**A field shared by more than one endpoint/fragment (the `hypnogram` precedent, `mezo-fk9a`).** When the new shape is referenced from more than one response/request (`SleepShotDraftResponse` **and** `LogSleepRequest`/`SleepLogResponse`), define it **once** as a named schema in `api/common/common-schemas.yml` and `$ref` it from every fragment that needs it, rather than duplicating the object inline per-fragment — the same pattern `SystemMessage`/`SystemMessageList` already use for the error contract. A `jsonb`-backed field additionally needs its own **typed embedded record** on the entity (`@JdbcTypeCode(SqlTypes.JSON)`, e.g. `SleepHypnogram`) rather than a raw `String` column, and — if the record's name would otherwise collide with the generated `api.dto.*` model — an explicit MapStruct `default` mapping method (`SleepLogMapper.map(SleepHypnogram)`) rather than relying on the generator's implicit conversion.

### People is Phase-2 backed (Slice E, `mezo-t16y.2`) — extend it here
The promotion shipped exactly per the old recipe: `api/feature/people/people.yml`, the `feature/people/{entity,repository,service,controller,mapper}` package (repositories on `JpaRepository` — person is date-less, mention orders by `ts`), Liquibase `person`/`mention`, `Person`/`MentionPopulator` + the `ResetDatabase` TRUNCATE additions, and the dual-mode `usePeople` (`MentionLogInput` was the pinned POST shape, `personId` rides the path). To add a mention capture surface (voice/text) beyond the chip sheet, widen `LogMentionRequest` further (source/duration/tiedTo/contextLabel are already columns + response fields).

### Swap a mock-only hook to real (the pattern)
`useWeight`/`useSleep` are the templates: a `useQuery` with `queryFn = mock ? () => mockSeed : api.list`, `initialData` only in mock; a `useMutation` whose `onSuccess` does `setQueryData` in mock and `invalidateQueries` in real. Keep the hook's returned shape unchanged so views don't move. (`useGoal` is the *read-only* variant — a `useQuery` with no mutation yet.)

### Add a Growth-page badge (Kitüntetések tab)
The 9 badges are **derived on read** in the backend `AchievementService` (an ordered `badge(key, icon, name, current, target)` list — [growth.md §3/§9](growth.md)), so a new badge is **backend code + contract only, no DDL and no FE change** (the `BadgesCard` renders whatever `AchievementsResponse.badges[]` carries — icon/name/achieved/current/target, generic 3-column grid). Add the badge row to the service (its `current` sourced from an EXISTING seam — `QuestLedgerSource`/`ActivityLedgerSource`/`TraitCalculator`/`SkillProgressRepository`, no new port), extend `AchievementsApiIT`, and update the mock seed `data/progression/achievementsMock.ts` + the MSW default so both FE modes stay green. Unlock **dates** are deliberately absent in v1 (a persistent achievement table with dates + a celebration overlay is the recorded future upgrade).

### Goal write path
The **Goal backend is live**: contract `api/feature/goal/goal.yml`, `feature/goal/`, and `goalApi` cover list/get/create/update/remove/activate/archive, while `useGoalCreation` drives the two-step planner. `useGoalActions()` owns archive/remove/activate/attach/detach; `EditGoalSheet` keeps archive/delete and read-only goal fields. Plan attach/detach is now rendered only on `GoalPlansPage` through `GoalConnectionTimeline` + `AttachPlanSheet`; the retired inline `GoalTimeline` and `GoalPlanSlots` components are deleted. The POST is protected by server-side ownership, archived, duplicate, same-type-overlap and goal-window invariants, clips only a valid tail crossing the goal end, then recomputes the prescription. The `toGoal` adapter and legacy timeline cache remain for compatibility while overview-native consumers continue replacing them. The **existing-goal field editor** (title/weights/dates write-back) remains deferred. The biometric profile is owned by the Profile biometrics flow, not the goal wizard.

Obligations that apply to every change here: **contract-first** for any boundary DTO, **dual-mode** parity (mock + real), and **both FE test modes green** plus the backend ITs. House standards: **[`docs/references/`](../references/)** (`java_package_structure`, `spring_patterns`, `error_handling`, `liquibase_conventions`, `testing_standards`, `integration_test_framework`, `configuration_conventions`, `api_contract_conventions`).

Key decisions: **the 1–10 scale correction** — the original brief's default `DayScoreService` formula assumed a 1–5 span for sleep quality and check-in energy; the actual FE dials (`SleepLogSheet`, `CheckInSheet`) are both 1–10, so the shipped normalization is `(v−1)/9`, verified against the FE code and pinned in `DayScoreServiceIT` rather than trusted from the spec; **the `<2`-subscore honesty gate is applied twice** — once per day (`DayScoreService.overallScore`) and once per week (`WeeklyScoreService.aggregate`, formerly `MeWeekService.roundedMeanOrNull`) — so a thin week never averages two "tanulom" days into a fabricated number; **the weekly-review generator runs BACKWARD** (Monday 06:50, for the week that just finished) unlike the pre-existing `WeeklySuggestionJob` (Monday 06:00, forward-looking for the current week) — the two Monday jobs are deliberately not merged, since one narrates the past and the other plans the future; **no lazy generation on the review GET** — unlike most proactive reads, `WeeklyReviewController` returns a straight 404 when the job hasn't produced a row yet (the job owns generation; a lazy GET would let a chatty client's GET race the cron and generate off a still-in-progress week); **the chat-handoff kickoff is never a persisted user message** — `ChatService.openingTurn`'s fixed Hungarian prompt only ever produces an assistant row, so the transcript reads as Mezo genuinely speaking first, not answering a hidden scripted question.

### Design 2.0 / Mozaik 2.0 (`mezo-d20`, 2026-08-29)

- **The Én tab is a hub, not a shell — [ADR 0032](../decisions/0032-five-tab-ia-dissolved-section-shells.md).** Five first-class tabs (`Nap · Edzés · Fuel · Mezo · Én`) + a floating coral quick-log FAB; every tab renders a Mozaik hub whose tiles open **full-page siblings**. No `<Outlet>`, no `SubNavDropdown`, no nested shells anywhere in Me. `MeSection.tsx`, `pages/tabs.ts`, `AppHero.tsx` and `SubNavDropdown.tsx` are deleted. **Nothing about the routes moved** — `/me/growth`, `/me/week`, `/me/weight`, `/me/sleep`, `/me/people`, `/me/knowledge`, `/me/ertesitesek`, `/me/ai-usage` are exactly where they were, which is why Me needed no redirect at all (only `/today/*`→`/nap` and `/insights/*`→`/mezo` did, via `LegacyPathRedirect` in `router.tsx`). **`/me/goals` is the one exception, and it moved later:** `mezo-iizd.1` Task 8 relocated the body-weight goal to `/me/goals/weight` and repurposed `/me/goals` as the `Célok` life-goals hub — see [`lifegoal.md`](lifegoal.md) and this doc's §2 `Célok`/`Cél` rows.
- **The visual language is the tile mosaic — [ADR 0033](../decisions/0033-mozaik-2-tile-language.md), which supersedes ADR 0026.** Tiles + clay 3D SVG icons (emoji retired as the icon vocabulary), the `--mz-*` token family, a one-shot entrance choreography and then stillness. Me is a pure consumer of `@/shared/ui/{mozaik,clay}`; it owns no primitive.
- **Tile lines are read from the destination page's own hook, never from a summary endpoint.** No aggregate read was added for the hub, and none should be: a tile that reports a number the page behind it has not read is exactly the `mezo-yew`/`mezo-0xl` bug class. The cost is the sixteen-hook cold load noted in §3; the benefit is that a tile and its page cannot disagree.
- **Absent beats zero.** A tile line is `undefined` while its source is pending/degraded/empty, and the tile renders with no line at all — never `0`, never `—`. `—` stays reserved for a *null statistic inside a stat cell* (the `Tempó`/`ETA` mini-cells), where the label has already promised a number. The `Heti` tile's „tanulom" is the single worded exception and it borrows the Heti hub's own vocabulary (`DAY_COPY`, `features/me/logic/weekDay.ts`).
- **The re-face was required to preserve behavior verbatim, and it did.** The `maintain` no-track guard, the `isComplete` goal-creation gate, the `<2`-day honesty gate, `formatRollupCost`'s sub-half-cent rule, the affect ring driven by the person's real `affectTrend`, the archive-updates-every-counter rule on the Tudás page — all carried over unchanged and pinned by the new page tests (§8).
