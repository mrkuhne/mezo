# ADR 0025 — Today's sky of islands becomes a napszak-tab switcher + a full-bleed mezo-sáv + a dobozolatlan nézet

- **Status:** Superseded by ADR 0026 (2026-08-11)
- **Driving bd:** `mezo-puci`
- **Spec:** [`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-10-today-daypart-tabs-design.md`](../superpowers/specs/2026-08-10-today-daypart-tabs-design.md) (+ the validated mockup [`assets/2026-08-10-today-daypart-tabs-mockup.html`](../superpowers/specs/assets/2026-08-10-today-daypart-tabs-mockup.html))
- **Supersedes:** the *render layer* of [ADR 0022](0022-today-three-islands.md) (the sky of three islands — capsule↔big morph, L0/L1 layering, the anchor-melt sky). ADR 0014's day model, card language, act-anywhere and dedup decisions stay in force unchanged, as does [ADR 0010](0010-gamified-growth-xp-feedback-not-payment.md) (nothing self-completes) — this is the render layer's **third** iteration over the same unchanged foundation (`dayFace.ts`, `todayItems.ts`, `islandFacts.ts`, `questAction`/`habitAction`, `windDown.ts`).

## Context

ADR 0022's sky of three islands optimised for **hiding**: exactly one island big at a time, and even the big island showed only a hero, 1–2 fact cells and one CTA — the briefing, the habits, and every todo sat one layer deeper behind an L1 unfold. In daily use this meant **two interactions** (pick an island → tap `még N ›`) before a single routine step could be acted on, and the companion's message was **invisible by default** — it only appeared as the L1 head, behind that same second tap. The user's own read of the shipped islands (the driving signal for this ADR, same as ADR 0024's Keret-hero one day earlier) was that this rejection-by-default was wrong for the app's most-visited screen: nothing about `/today` should require a discovery step.

## Decision

`/today` becomes a normally-scrolling page (`.screen-content`'s own scroll — the sky's non-scrolling `:has()` flex-flip is dropped from Today) with, top to bottom: `AppHero` → `VulnerabilityCard?` → **`DaypartTabs`** (a `.segtabs` switcher, the house control already used by Sport/Futás — not a new switcher language) → **`MezoMessage`** (a full-bleed `CoachBubble` band) → the selected daypart's **complete content, with no outer card frame** (`DaypartPanel`).

1. **`DaypartTabs`** — three segments (🌅 Reggel · ☀️ Nap · 🌙 Este), `role="group" aria-label="Napszak"`, `aria-pressed` per segment. The chronologically-current daypart carries a small gold **`.daytab-now`** dot **independent of the pressed segment** — the ADR 0014 dual-signal ("hol tartok" vs "mit nézek") inherited verbatim. `?dp=reggel|nap|este` stays the single source of the selection — URL-derived, never mirrored into state; `null`/`''`/unknown fall back to the clock's daypart; selecting the current daypart **deletes** the param; every write uses `{ replace: true }` (unchanged `TodayPage` logic, now literally verbatim — see [today.md §2](../features/today.md)). A tab switch scrolls `.screen-content` to the top (the successor to the old "face switch closes L1" rule — there is no L1 to close anymore).
2. **`MezoMessage`** — the `BriefingCard` successor: the same `CoachBubble`, but **full-bleed** (`margin: 0`, no left rule, no radius), **no avatar** (the eyebrow carries identity), and **never truncated** — no 3-line clamp, no `bővebben`. It renders **once**, above the tabs' content, **always the day's briefing regardless of which daypart is selected**. The day/evening companion note is not demoted — it still renders, but inside its *own* daypart's panel, above that daypart's group list, so the two coach voices never compete on one line.
3. **`DaypartPanel`** — the frameless shell: no card, no `surface-1` background, no shadow, no halo blob. Content sits directly on the canvas — boxes only survive where they were already load-bearing (the fact strip, `ItemRow`, the warn-chip, the creed-chip). Anatomy: a left-aligned hero number (`DaypartHero`, Geist 200, ~30px, no more centred blob-numeral), the unchanged `IslandFactsStrip`, an optional warn-chip/CTA row, an optional companion-note head, the groups (`DayGroups` — the `IslandList` job minus its internal scroller and its `összecsuk` toggle), and the **one surviving collapsed element**: the done-fold (`✓ N kész · +M XP ▾`). The evening daypart's four `windDown` phases (normál → ráhangolódás → leállás → éjszaka) play out in this same hero/CTA band instead of a big-island hero block; the éjszaka phase darkens the **panel** itself (`DaypartPanel`'s `night` prop, `.dayview.is-night` — theme-invariant dark, the `.isl-night`/`.wdb-night` heritage) since there is no card left to darken.
4. **Rough day (`?day=rough`)** keeps its exact semantics and guard order (`anchorMode` checked first, synchronous off the URL, no skeleton flash) — only the container changes: `AnchorIsland`'s content is unchanged, now wrapped in a `DaypartPanel` with a **constant `tone="reggel"`**, never the clock-derived `current`. This is deliberate, not an oversight: `DaypartPanel` puts `key={tone}` on its root to cross-fade a daypart switch, and `current` is re-derived from `new Date()` on every render — a clock-derived tone risks flipping the `key` mid-session (e.g. a focus refetch crossing a daypart boundary) and remounting the melt, silently discarding `AnchorIsland`'s local tick state, which is persisted nowhere. The melt is not a daypart, so it has no tone of its own to be right about; the fix is to keep it constant.
5. **Motion** shrinks to what the new layout still needs: the capsule buborék-morph (`isl-morph`), the capsule float (`isl-floaty`) and the L1 stagger ladder (`isl-rowin`) leave Today outright — there is no capsule and no layer to unfold. What remains: a daypart-switch cross-fade + 8px rise (`isl-phasein`, reused verbatim — not a new keyframe), the evening phase cross-fade (same keyframe), and the done-fold's height animation. The `:where()`-guarded cascade rule (the reduce-block must win the source-order tie-break) still applies and is still asserted structurally by `todayReducedMotion.test.ts`, re-anchored onto the surviving families.

## Consequences

- **The capsule buborék-morph motion language is gone from Today.** There is no more "select grows a capsule into a big island" moment — `isl-morph`/`isl-floaty`/`isl-rowin` have no Today consumer left (they survive for Fuel, see below). This was the single most distinctive piece of ADR 0022's identity; it is a deliberate trade for immediacy over spectacle.
- **The daypart colour identity moves from content to chrome.** Without a blob there is no per-daypart tint on the content itself; the daypart's identity is now carried by the **tab** (emoji + the pressed segment's primary tint) and the app-wide circadian canvas (`[data-day]`), unchanged. Accepted loss, same call as the spec's §8: the chrome hordozza a napszak-jelölést, nem a tartalom.
- **`shared/ui/Island` and almost the whole `.isl-*` CSS family SURVIVE — but now belong to Fuel alone.** `TodayPage.tsx` no longer imports or builds `IslandCapsule`s; its only remaining `.isl-*` footprint is `AnchorIsland.tsx` reusing a handful of individual classes directly (`isl-openhead`, `isl-hero-v/-u/-sub`, `isl-anchor-rows`, `isl-act`, `isl-more`) as plain content styling, not the capsule↔big shell. Every other `.isl-*` consumer is Fuel's `WindowIsland`/`FuelMaiPage` (`shared/ui/Island.tsx`'s sole remaining shell caller). This means **leaving Today did NOT bring a CSS cleanup** — the family that shipped for Today's own three islands is now entirely someone else's, and only a handful of rules that had no life outside Today's old big-island were deletable: `.isl-doneline`, `.isl-nightrow`, `.isl-nightrow-arr`, `.isl-phase`, plus the `.isl-anchor`/`.sky-islands.is-anchor` block that `IslandSky`'s anchor-melt state alone emitted (`.isl.isl-anchor`, `.sky-islands.is-anchor`, `.sky-islands.is-anchor .isl:not(.isl-anchor)`, `.sky-islands.is-anchor .isl-anchor`, `.isl-anchor .isl-blob` — caught and removed in the final review pass). We accept this: `.isl-*` was already promoted to `shared/ui` for Fuel a branch ago ([ADR 0023](0023-fuel-window-river.md)) — Today merely stops being its second consumer, it does not own an obligation to prune vocabulary another feature still needs.
- **New Today-owned CSS, small and frameless by design:** `.daytabs`/`.daytab-now` (the tab switcher), `.coach-bubble.cb-band` (the full-bleed message band modifier), `.dayview`/`.dv-*` (the frameless panel — `.dv-hero*`, `.dv-act`, `.dv-done*`, `.dv-nightrow*`, `.dv-state`). These deliberately do **not** duplicate the micro-components the panel still needs — `.isl-facts`/`.isl-fact*`, `.isl-warnchip`, `.isl-cta`, `.isl-grouph` render inside `.dayview` unchanged, scoped with `.dayview .isl-*` compound selectors rather than copied under `.dv-*`.
- **Fewer interactions to act.** The entire selected daypart's content — hero, facts, every group, every row — is visible without an unfold; the only collapsed element left anywhere on the page is the done-fold. This is the whole point of the ADR: zero-to-one interaction for any routine step, one always for anything already done.
- **The evening island's four `windDown` phases and the offered-exactly-once `wind_down` habit rule are unchanged**, just replayed in the panel's hero/CTA band instead of a big-island hero block — `useWindDownPhase` and `windDown.ts` were not touched.
- **Retired outright, component + test:** `IslandSky`, `IslandMorning`, `IslandDay`, `IslandEvening`, `IslandList`, `BriefingCard` (+ their tests). `TodaySkeleton` was rebuilt to mirror the new tab-row + message-band + panel layout instead of the island-shaped mirror; the `.apphero` same-DOM-node identity contract survives unchanged.
- **The day model underneath is, for the third time, completely unchanged** — `logic/dayFace.ts`, `logic/todayItems.ts`, `logic/islandFacts.ts`, `questAction.ts`, `habitAction.ts`, `windDown.ts`/`useWindDownPhase.ts` carry no commits in this branch's range; the `act()` dispatch table, `servableAction`, the seven sheets, the consume-once level-ups and the guard order (`anchorMode` first, then `sleepGoalPending`) are all verbatim inherited.

## Alternatives considered

- **(a) Three stacked sections in one scroll** (Reggel content, then Nap content, then Este content, one long page). Rejected: turns `/today` into a long page with no daypart focus — the user has to scroll past two dayparts' worth of content to reach the one they came for, and "which daypart am I looking at" blurs the moment two sections are simultaneously on screen.
- **(b) Force the island shell into an "all-three-big" mode** (keep `shared/ui/Island`, just render all three permanently expanded). Rejected: the shell is Fuel's too now, and its entire reason to exist is the capsule↔big morph — an island that is never small is not an island, it is a card wearing an island's CSS for no reason. Building a new frameless shell (`DaypartPanel`) instead of repurposing `Island` keeps Fuel's shell free to evolve for its own capsule-morph needs without Today's frameless requirements pulling on it.
