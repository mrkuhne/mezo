## Decisions taken while planning (differences from the prototype — owner informed in the plan hand-off)

1. **Test tab keeps the two existing URLs.** Tab route `/me/weight` (default view, owner decision 6) owning `/me/sleep`; the Súly | Alvás switch navigates between them. No redirect, every existing deep link (`habitAction.ts`, `questAction.ts`, backend `/me/sleep`) keeps working untouched. Supersedes the spec's `/me/test?nezet=` sketch.
2. **Heted hero lines (owner chose B, 2026-10-05).** The weekly review gains two model-written fields, `wentWell` and `watchOut` (slice SB), shown as „Jól ment: …" / „Figyelj rá: …" exactly as in the prototype. Rows generated before SB carry `null`: the hero then falls back to the **first two sentences of the review summary** as plain lines (no labels). No review → the existing honest sentence from `analysisSnippet`. Guardrails: each field is stripped, blank → `null`, longer than 160 characters → `null`, and a numeral not present in the week payload (`ProseNumberGuard`) → `null`. A rejected field never drops the review — only a blank `summary` does.
3. **Életvonal stations** come from two stored sources only: the weekly average first crossing a whole kilogram toward the goal ("80 kg alatt"), and perks unlocked in the window (`useAchievements().perks[].unlockedAt`). Badges/titles carry no date, so "Kitüntetés: A kitartó" from the prototype is not derivable and is not shown.
4. **Biometrics door.** The hub's bio line (`34 év · 180 cm · …` → `/settings/me/biometrics`) moves to a quiet flat row at the bottom of both Test views (it is body data). Added to the prototype in Task 1.
