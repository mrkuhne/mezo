Replace the editorial serif treatment with rounded sans typography, warm ivory surfaces, restrained luminous accents and soft controls. Only the standalone prototype changes. Earlier comparison studies remain available. Existing check-ins, domain navigation, chat continuity and sport-linked food budget must still work.
