| 15 | 2026-11-13 | The Pénz area — shape, horizons and where it is built (`mezo-r36n9.33.1`) | **Migrate the finance app into Boop; five pages; four horizons** | The standalone Base44 finance app is replaced, data and all: its logic moves into the Mezo backend and its screens become native Boop pages. | 16 | 2026-11-13 | What Boop may and may not say about money (`mezo-r36n9.33.1`) | **Concrete products with two or three options — and six hard rules** | Rejected: categories only („40% biztonságos, 35% széles részvénypiac") — it leaves him the exact work he wanted to avoid. So Boop names concrete products, but **never one name: two or three per sleeve, with what each is, how they differ, what they cost, and the why.** The six rules every Pénz string is written to: Boop **never buys or moves money** (he acts, Boop records) · **never urges** (no "act now", no countdowns, no streaks on money) · **never promises a return** · **never says sell** (it explains a fall, it does not send him to sell) · **„ma nincs dolgod" is a complete answer**, and most days it is the answer · **always says why, and where it could be wrong.** The architectural consequence, from the research on numeric hallucination: **the model narrates figures computed by the backend and passed through verbatim**, each with a citation and a date stamp — no free-text numeric answers about the portfolio, and the old app's amount guards, prompt-injection labelling of feed text and SSRF-guarded fetch are ported and extended. Perimeter: this sits outside regulated investment advice only while the app serves one person; sharing it with anyone is a new decision, not a deployment detail. |
