# Pénz area — prior art for the AI investing companion

Recon for the Pénz brainstorm (2026-11-13, `mezo-r36n9.33`). External sources only; the old finance
app is mapped in [`02-finance-tracker-map.md`](02-finance-tracker-map.md). Everything here is
*input to a design*, not a design.

## 1. Keeping long-horizon holdings out of the daily noise — M1 "News Snapshot"

M1's News Snapshot ([announcement](https://m1.com/blog/introducing-news-snapshot/),
[help](https://help.m1.com/en/articles/12315347-news-snapshot)) takes the user's **top holdings only**,
groups articles about the same story from several sources into one item, and runs a published
**relevance check**: will it matter in years or decades · does it change industry structure · is it a
lasting regulatory or technological change · does it meaningfully affect long-run financials · does it
change the company's strategic position. "Reactionary, short-term news" is explicitly excluded. Two
sections: **Portfolio focus** and **Macro Lens**. Each story shows a tappable source count.

Two lessons. The criteria are published and copyable. And a serious product chose **weekly, not
daily**, for the long horizon — the cadence *is* the filter. Weak spot: an all-ETF portfolio leaves
Portfolio focus empty.

## 2. Non-broker portfolio trackers (feature floor)

Sharesight (mature, performance attribution + tax, dearest), Snowball Analytics (dividend forecast,
EU-friendly), Stock Events (cheapest, has a public API). All are manual/CSV/broker-import ledgers with
analytics on top; reviews agree **corporate actions need manual reconciliation** — a real running cost
of any self-built holdings ledger. The one idea here that suits a daily life dashboard is the
**forward-looking portfolio event timeline** (earnings, ex-dividend dates). None of them solves
multi-horizon framing, and none has a usable "today" screen.
(Sources are vendor-adjacent comparisons; pricing conflicts between them — verify before quoting.)

## 3. Buckets / sleeves — strong theory, no app prior art

The bucket strategy splits one portfolio by **when the money will be used**, each tier with its own
risk and its own refill rule ([Kitces](https://www.kitces.com/?p=538),
[Avantis](https://avantisinvestors.com/avantis-insights/understanding-the-behavioral-advantage-and-trade-offs-of-bucket-portfolios),
[Morningstar](https://morningstar.ca/ca/news/188087/5-misconceptions-about-the-bucket-approach-.aspx)).
The claim is **behavioural, not financial**: the near bucket visibly covering the next N years is what
stops the owner selling the long one in a drawdown. Counter-points: it is mental accounting, it costs
upkeep, it matters far less while still accumulating, and 6–7 buckets become a job.

This gives the honest framing for "performance per bucket without encouraging churn": **the long
bucket's number is years of coverage, not today's per cent.** The researcher found **no concrete app
UX** for bucket views — that part is original work.

## 4. AI + market news: the failure modes are numeric

- Domain fine-tuning on financial text made numeric hallucination **worse**, with reported overt
  rates rising from ~5 % to 80–90 % while the prose got more professional-sounding
  ([Neurocomputing abstract](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0925231226024094);
  figures via snippet — unverified).
- Multi-document summarisation reports up to 75 % hallucinated content in adverse setups
  ([NAACL 2025 findings](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-naacl.293.pdf)) — and a market digest
  *is* multi-document.
- The mitigation that recurs: **compute every number outside the model and pass it through verbatim**.
- Usable error taxonomy for evaluation: **Entity Swap · Numerical Error · Negation Flip · Temporal
  Confusion** (wrong company, wrong number, inverted claim, stale date).
- RAG is not a cure: repetition and hallucination worsen at large retrieval k.

**Architectural rule this implies:** prices, P&L and percentages are never generated by the model; it
narrates figures computed elsewhere, each with a citation and a date stamp. No free-text numeric
answers about the portfolio. This matches, and extends, the amount-guard machinery the old app already
has (`02-finance-tracker-map.md` §5).

## 5. Day trading without execution or a live tape — the journal literature

([Tradervue](https://www.tradervue.com/site/2022/08/19/trading-best-practices-and-tips/),
[JournalPlus on tagging](https://journalplus.co/blog/how-to-tag-trades-effectively),
[R-multiple](https://tapeboard.com/methodology/r-multiple))

1. **One trade ID per position, not per fill** — splitting scale-ins corrupts holding period, R and win rate.
2. **Setup tags + a mistake flag**, with a deliberately small vocabulary: 8 tags on 98 % of trades beat
   20 tags on 60 %, because sparse tags destroy the analytics.
3. **R-multiple** (P/L ÷ initial risk) as the unit of review, so position sizing stops hiding the truth.
   Expectancy framing: 40 % win rate at +2.5R / −1R is +0.5R per trade.
4. A **review ritual** separate from the journal: win rate by setup · is the R distribution healthy
   (a few +2R…+4R, many small −1R, nothing worse than ≈−1.5R) · this week's most common mistake and
   what it cost. "A journal without reviews is just a spreadsheet."
5. **Sample-size honesty**: under ~50 tagged trades, win rate is noise.

All of this needs only entry, exit and stop — typed by the user. It is the realistic shape of
day-trade support here: **plan before · journal after · review weekly**.

## 6. Market data and news

**EODHD** ([pricing](https://eodhd.com/pricing), read first-hand): free sandbox 20 calls/day,
15-minute delay; Historian $19.99/mo (EOD + live, 100k calls/day); ALL-IN-ONE $99.99/mo. Call
weighting: EOD/live 1 · **news 5** · fundamentals 10 · bulk 100+. **News + sentiment is on every plan,
including free.** EU breadth demonstrated (LSE, XETRA, Warsaw, a EUFUND virtual exchange for European
funds). **Budapest / HUF coverage is not confirmed** — settle it against their
[exchange list](https://eodhd.com/financial-apis/list-supported-exchanges) before anything depends on
it. Prices are aggregated VWAP from many sources, not exchange feeds; redistribution terms are not on
the pricing page.

Free tiers (third-party comparisons, figures conflict — verify): Alpha Vantage ≈25 requests/day,
Finnhub ≈60 calls/min with a year of company news, Twelve Data ≈800 requests/day (delayed), Marketstack
100 requests/**month**. Several free tiers are **non-commercial use only**; real-time generally needs
exchange licences. The old app's free path — Yahoo chart endpoint + RSS + frankfurter.dev for FX —
already works and costs nothing.

## 7. Where the advice line runs

ESMA's 2023 supervisory briefing on the
[definition of advice](https://www.esma.europa.eu/press-news/esma-news/esma-updates-its-guidance-definition-advice-supervisory-briefing):
investment advice is a **personal recommendation to a client** about transactions in financial
instruments — "personal" meaning presented as suitable for that person or based on their
circumstances. Information published to the public is not advice, but ESMA flags that apps and
websites may be judged differently, **disclaimers do not settle it**, and implicit recommendations,
model portfolios and copy trading can qualify.

The definition hinges on there being a **client**. A tool one person builds and uses alone, serving
nobody else, is outside the regulated activity. MNB (Hungary) material confirming this was not found —
**unverified**. The perimeter changes the moment the app is shared with anyone else.

## What carries the most weight

1. **M1's relevance criteria** — the only real prior art for keeping long-horizon holdings out of the
   daily noise, and it chose weekly for exactly that reason.
2. **The numeric-hallucination evidence** — the AI narrates, it never computes.
3. **The trade-journal primitives** — a complete, useful day-trade feature set with no execution and no
   live tape.
4. **Buckets have theory but no UX prior art** — expect to design it.
5. **EODHD is the one verified EU-broad source with news on the free tier; HUF coverage is open.**
